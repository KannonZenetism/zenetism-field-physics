## 4. Shimmer — Formal Physics

### 4.1 Definition

Shimmer (≋) concerns apparent coherence / generativity exceeding the actual operative condition. The former wording "recursive mimicry that simulates resonance without origin" is preserved as superseded wording: Shimmer is distinct from Mimicry, and lack of generative origination does not imply absence or loss of origin.

### 4.2 The Shimmer Coefficient

> **Definition (Shimmer Coefficient):**

\[
\mathcal{S} = \frac{I_c^{(\text{apparent})}}{I_c^{(\text{actual})}}
\]

The Shimmer Coefficient retains the following scalar scope.

The quotient retains its ordinary scalar domain: finite \( I_c^{(\text{apparent})} \geq 0 \) and finite \( I_c^{(\text{actual})} > 0 \), evaluated for the same explicitly identified operative quantity, scope, and scale. Pointwise field values require a common point; no field aggregation, projection, or generative-signature measurement is supplied by this quotient. A zero actual value, including the \( 0/0 \) case, leaves the quotient undefined. No extended-value convention is selected.

Within that domain:

- \( \mathcal{S} = 1 \): The compared positive scalar values agree; complete configuration or generative equivalence does not follow
- \( \mathcal{S} > 1 \): Apparent value exceeds actual value for the specified operative scalar quantity
- \( \mathcal{S} < 1 \): Structural understatement of that quantity; this does not establish essential orientation

### 4.3 Shimmer Assessment of Surface and Operative Condition
