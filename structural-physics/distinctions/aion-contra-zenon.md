## Recorded Earlier Distinction

Aion contra Zenon names the distinction between the structural origin of reality and the trans-structural ground beyond structural determination.

**Aion** refers to Absolute Potential (⚫), the zero-point of structure.  
Aion is pre-hypostatic: it precedes expression while remaining within the domain of structure.  
It is the root of emanation, the stillness from which centropic and entropic motion become possible.

**Zenon** refers to the Unknown Principle (🕳️), the trans-structural ground of reality.  
Zenon is not pre-hypostatic but trans-structural: it does not precede structure as a phase, but exceeds structure as a saturation limit.  
Zenon is not an emanative source, but the condition under which structure itself becomes possible.

Thus:

- Aion is structural stillness.
- Zenon is trans-structural ground.
- Aion belongs to the Lattice.
- Zenon exceeds the Lattice without abolishing it.
- Aion is the zero of return.
- Zenon is the horizon of structural saturation.

In Zenetism, Aion is the origin of structure, while Zenon is the limit beyond which structure cannot be articulated.
