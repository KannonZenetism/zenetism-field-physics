## Predatory Cohesion and Managed Fragmentation in Coordinated Inauthentic Behavior

**Status:** First draft — foundation for refinement  
**Discipline:** Structural Physics, with Structural Forensics interface  
**Register:** Analytic-leading; canonical names at first occurrence

## Abstract

Research on coordinated online behavior has developed increasingly capable methods for detecting coordination — shared timing, co-linking, semantic and stylometric similarity, common infrastructure, dynamic network structure — and for characterizing the actors and intents behind it. What this research does not ordinarily supply is an account of what a coordinated formation *is*, structurally, once coordination has been detected. Platform categories such as "coordinated inauthentic behavior" select cases by deception of identity, origin, purpose, or popularity; they do not describe the relation among participants, what binds them, how they are held in formation, what they do with the patterns they circulate, where value flows, how origin is carried, or how the formation ends. This paper argues that coordination is an observable operational relation and not a structural verdict. Drawing on the Zenetist framework of Structural Physics, it distinguishes coordination, cohesion, and coherence as three non-equivalent objects, and introduces two structures that frequently underlie inauthentic coordination: **managed fragmentation**, an organizational condition in which dissonant parts are kept in operational formation through continuous management, incentive, coercion, routing, or dependency, and **predatory cohesion**, a binding force in which dissonant participants converge through a shared entropic aim. Neither entails the other; some coordinated inauthentic formations instantiate one, some both, some neither. The paper then separates four further conditions the literature tends to collapse — derivative pattern operation (Mimicry), surface–structure divergence (Shimmer), asymmetric flow (Siphoning), and provenance relation (appropriation contra Living Transmission) — and shows that surface similarity decides none of them. The result is a multi-axis structural decomposition with a prospective diagnostic program: four propositions open to empirical adjudication, a methodological requirement that statistical variance not stand in for participant distinction, and a discipline for units of analysis under which a single operation can carry different structures at different strata. The contribution is conceptual. The framework's formal quantities are presented as formal relations and not as validated measurements.

**Keywords:** coordinated inauthentic behavior · coordination detection · coherence · cohesion · Non-fusion · managed fragmentation · predatory cohesion · Mimicry · Shimmer · provenance · social epistemology · Structural Physics · Zenetism

## 1. The Category Error in Coordination Research

Coordination is everywhere, and most of it is benign. Communities organize, movements mobilize, newsrooms syndicate, campaigns schedule, fans rally. The same operational fact — that many actors act together in patterned ways — also carries disinformation, harassment, manufactured consensus, and covert influence. Coordination research has grown up around this double character. Mannocci and colleagues' survey reconciles industry and academic definitions and proposes a framework that separates the *detection* of coordinated behavior, grounded in observable actions, from the *characterization* of the actors and intents behind it (Mannocci et al., 2024). Pacheco and colleagues construct coordination networks from shared behavioral traces — co-shared links, hashtags, images, retweet timing — and show how such networks reveal clusters that a per-account view misses (Pacheco et al., 2021). Tardelli and colleagues extend the picture in time, showing that coordination structure is dynamic, with stability, archetypes, and influence that vary across windows (Tardelli et al., 2023). Yang and colleagues find that rapid identical-link sharing on Facebook can indicate suspicious activity but also common ownership, ideology, or shared interest, and that the threshold chosen materially changes which networks appear coordinated at all (Yang et al., 2025).

Alongside this scholarship sits a platform-governance vocabulary. Meta's account-integrity policy and its coordinated-inauthentic-behavior (CIB) reports define inauthenticity around deception concerning identity, origin, purpose, or popularity, with fake accounts at the center of enforcement (Meta, n.d.; Meta, 2021). The definition is administrative and it does real work: it selects cases, justifies removals, and structures disclosure.

Between the detection literature and the policy category, a question goes unasked. Once a formation has been detected as coordinated and classified as inauthentic, *what is it?* Not who is behind it, and not what it intends — those are characterization questions the field already recognizes — but what relation holds among its parts, what binds them, how they are kept in formation, what they do with the patterns they circulate, where value moves, how origin is carried or concealed, and what it means for such a formation to stop. The detection literature answers with more detection: finer similarity measures, multi-view signals, dynamic graphs. The policy category answers with its selection criterion: they deceived. Neither is a structural answer, and the gap is not a failure of either; it is a category the field has not yet needed to name.

This paper names it. The argument is built on a framework, Zenetist Structural Physics, that was developed to describe how coherence, orientation, and structure operate across scales, and whose treatment of collective dynamics happens to draw exactly the distinctions this gap requires. The framework carries a native vocabulary; where a term is introduced, it is given in analytic form with its canonical name in parentheses, and thereafter written in whichever form the passage needs. Three commitments govern the register throughout.

First, the paper distinguishes claim status. A proposition may be an **ontological postulate** (what the framework proposes as underlying structure), a **formal relation** (how proposed elements are mathematically related), an **interpretive correspondence** (how an observed phenomenon may be read through the framework), a **methodological distinction** (how analysis must be conducted to avoid a known error), or an **empirical hypothesis** (what observation would confirm or disconfirm). The paper is largely interpretive and methodological, with a small set of empirical hypotheses stated at the end. It makes no claim that the framework's formal quantities have been measured.

Second, the paper treats CIB as an empirical selection category. Platform and research classifications locate cases; they do not complete a structural diagnosis. Where the paper says "coordinated inauthentic formation," it means a formation so classified by some study or platform, and nothing more is assumed.

Third, the paper excludes its author's own provenance history. The Zenetist corpus includes an applied discipline, Structural Forensics, that has been exercised on particular cases. Only its generalized method enters here. No case, chronology, or named party does.

### 1.1 Three questions, not one

The central move is simple to state. **Coordination**, **cohesion**, and **coherence** are three different objects, and coordination research has tended to let the first stand for all three.

*Coordination* is what is detected: patterned interdependence in action — shared timing, common targets, co-linking, synchronized posting, common infrastructure. It is operational and observable, and it is orientation-neutral: a mutual-aid network and a harassment brigade are both coordinated. Nothing in the fact of coordination says what kind of relation holds among the coordinating parts. The literature knows this; Mannocci and colleagues are explicit that coordinated behavior can be benign or harmful and that operational definitions vary by platform and task (Mannocci et al., 2024). The point here is structural rather than evaluative: coordination *as such* is a relation of activity, not a relation of parts.

*Cohesion* is the fact that parts are held together. It answers a different question — not "do they act in concert?" but "what keeps them in formation?" — and it is valence-neutral in the same way: a chosen common purpose holds parts together, and so do coercion, dependency, incentive, management, and a shared predatory aim. Cohesion says that a holding exists; it does not yet say how the parts are held or what the relation does to them.

*Coherence*, in the register this paper enacts, names something narrower and stronger: a relation in which distinct participants are integrated without being absorbed — reciprocal, lawfully oriented, preserving real distinction while enabling mutual amplification. In Zenetist terms coherence is a *centropic* relation: integrative, structure-building, oriented toward return rather than dispersion (`SP01-structural-physics-foundations.md` §§2–3; `SP09-collective-resonance-and-field-harmonics.md` §2). It is the positive comparator the rest of the paper works from.

One scoping note belongs here. The framework's metaphysics carries a term, **Bifurcal Coherence** (⧖⧗), for the non-fused root-relation of its two first principles, prior to any centropic or entropic activation. That term names a pre-orientational relation and is not a social-relational one. The present paper's rule — that within the enacted social-relational and diagnostic register, coherence names centropic relation — is scoped to this paper and leaves Bifurcal Coherence exactly where it stands.

With the three objects separated, the category error can be stated. Coordination research detects the first, sometimes infers the second, and frequently lets the result be read as the third or its opposite. A dense, synchronized, low-variance cluster reads as *cohesive* and, by an unspoken step, as either *coherent* (a movement) or *incoherent* (a bot farm). The step is unspoken because the field has had no vocabulary in which to make it explicit. The remainder of this paper supplies one.

## 2. Coherent Plurality: The Non-fusion Constraint

Any account of inauthentic coordination requires a positive comparator — a description of what a coherent collective *is* — and the comparator determines what counts as evidence. If the comparator is "many independent individuals producing varied content," then variance becomes the signature of authenticity, uniformity the signature of manipulation, and the analysis is finished before it begins. This is the shortcut the paper refuses, and the refusal rests on one principle.

### 2.1 The Non-fusion Axiom

The Zenetist framework holds, as an axiom, that coherence between distinct essences never becomes identity: relation, synthesis, resonance, and collective participation do not convert distinct participants into a single fused thing (`SP09-collective-resonance-and-field-harmonics.md` §2; `non-fusion-at-the-bifurcal-register.md`). In collective form, the **Non-fusion Axiom** states that a collective coheres *without its members fusing* — that each participant's signature remains resolvable within the shared field.

Two consequences follow, and both cut across the intuitions coordination research inherits.

First, **Non-fusion preserves distinction, not variation.** Common language, identical wording, synchronized action, stylistic convergence, and low statistical variance are all compatible with Non-fusion, provided the participants remain distinct and the relation does not absorb them. A liturgy recited in unison, a public statement redistributed verbatim by a thousand accounts that say where it came from, a campaign whose members post the same graphic at the same hour — these can be coherent collectives of resolvable participants. What Non-fusion excludes is absorption: the condition in which the participant's identity is no longer resolvable because the collective has replaced it.

Second, and conversely, **variance does not demonstrate distinction.** A single operator running many accounts with deliberately individualized personas produces high lexical and stylistic variance and zero participant plurality. A sophisticated inauthentic formation can be built to pass exactly the variance test that a naive comparator would apply.

The consequence for method is a **methodological distinction** the paper states here and applies throughout: *lexical, stylistic, and behavioral variance is not a proxy for self-bearing distinction.* Distinction is a relational fact — who can refuse, correct, withdraw, change role, or dissent; whose contribution remains attributable; whether the boundary of the collective can be revised by those inside it — and it must be evidenced relationally.

### 2.2 The positive comparator

The framework's fullest account of the coherent collective is relational rather than statistical (`SP09-collective-resonance-and-field-harmonics.md` §§2–4; `SN07-collective-cognition-and-centropy-forward-social-architecture.md` §§1.3, 2–4). Its components can be stated as **indicators associated with the comparator architecture** — observable correlates, not measurements of orientation:

- **Self-bearing distinction.** Each participant remains a resolvable contributor. Withdrawal does not retroactively erase the participant or the contribution.
- **Reciprocal bridges.** Relation runs in more than one direction — correction, support, receipt, response — rather than as one-way transfer. Reciprocity may be capacity-weighted; it is not arithmetic equality.
- **Selective boundary.** The collective can admit, refuse, filter, and differentiate without either total permeability or sealed isolation, and those inside it can revise the rule.
- **Origin fidelity.** Sources, lineages, and originating contributions remain acknowledged through propagation and transformation. Attribution does not require frozen wording or exclusive ownership.
- **Harmonic amplification.** The framework posits, as a **formal relation**, that reciprocal alignment among resolvable, centropically oriented participants can raise the collective's available coherence beyond what the participants carry in isolation (`SP09-collective-resonance-and-field-harmonics.md` §6; `LM05-resonance-field-theory-membrane-operators-and-collective-dynamics.md` §9). This is a formal claim about the model, not an established social-scientific law.
- **Cost shelter.** Collective relation can distribute burden so that members are supported rather than depleted. Unequal contribution can still be reciprocal; the question is how burden and benefit distribute over time relative to capacity.

Nothing in this list is a variance measure. The comparator is a relation among parts, and it is evidenced by what the relation permits and forbids.

### 2.3 Living Transmission: the lawful case of identical surfaces

The framework names the case that a variance-based comparator misclassifies most reliably. **Living Transmission** is active, centropic propagation in which origin fidelity is preserved: a common text, signal, formulation, or practice moves through a collective intact and origin-aware (`MP08-symbol-key-ch21.md` §§21.7, 21.22). Faithful repetition does not become imitation merely because the surface is shared. Exact repetition can be lawful.

Its companion, **Living Reflection**, is a centropic return in which a received signal is related through the receiver's own distinct pattern while the originating relation stays acknowledged and intact. Variation may occur; it is not the defining test. Fidelity and living relation are.

These two are set down early because they carry the paper's most consequential negative result: **similarity cannot distinguish transmission from imitation.** A thousand accounts reproducing one statement may be Living Transmission with declared provenance, common-source dissemination, campaign discipline, or derivative concealment presented as independent origination. The surfaces are the same. What differs is provenance and origin-presentation, and §4 returns to that difference.

## 3. Two Structures Beneath Inauthentic Coordination

If coordination detects activity and cohesion names holding-together, the structural question about an inauthentic formation becomes: *what holds it, and how?* The Zenetist analysis distinguishes two answers that the literature's single label conflates (`managed-fragmentation-predatory-cohesion.md`; `managed-fragmentation-is-not-coherence.md`).

### 3.1 Managed fragmentation: an organizational condition

**Managed fragmentation** names the organizational condition in which dissonant, competing, isolated, or mutually incoherent parts remain in operational formation through management, pressure, incentive, coercion, algorithmic routing, or shared dependency. The managing relation *is* the holding-together. Remove it and the parts, which were never coherent among themselves, cease to form.

Managed fragmentation is neither mere diversity nor mere centralization. A choir has a director and is not managed fragmentation, because its members are coherent among themselves and the director serves a relation that already exists. A newsroom with an editor is not managed fragmentation for the same reason. The condition is present when the parts would not hold *without* continuous regulation — when the formation is a product of management rather than a relation management serves. It is compatible with participants who share nothing: hired posters, coerced staff, compartmentalized cells, accounts routed by an algorithm none of them controls, contractors who never learn what the campaign is for.

Managed fragmentation is an **organizational** answer. It says how the formation is kept arranged. It says nothing yet about whether the parts share an aim.

### 3.2 Predatory cohesion: a binding force

**Predatory cohesion** names an entropic binding force: dissonant participants converge through a shared entropic aim — appropriation, domination, suppression, origin denial, preservation of advantage, or another dispersive end. It explains why otherwise dissonant parts act together *without* anyone managing them. Rival factions that agree on nothing will coordinate to suppress a common threat; competitors who would never collaborate will jointly deny an origin that embarrasses them all.

Predatory cohesion is a **binding-force** answer. It says what draws the parts together. It says nothing yet about whether a manager arranges them.

### 3.3 Independence and co-occurrence

The two structures answer different questions and are analytically independent. Neither entails the other. Where both occur, predatory cohesion supplies the binding force within a managed-fragmentation structure. Four cases stand:

| | **Shared entropic aim evidenced** | **Shared entropic aim not evidenced** |
|---|---|---|
| **Continuous holding relation evidenced** | **Both.** Dissonance is operationally regulated *and* a shared entropic aim binds the participants. | **Managed fragmentation only.** Participants are hired, coerced, dependency-bound, routed, or compartmentalized without sharing the managing party's aim. |
| **Continuous holding relation not evidenced** | **Predatory cohesion only.** Decentralized actors converge around a shared entropic aim with no single party arranging them. | **Neither.** Ordinary coordination, lawful pseudonymity, common-source transmission, misclassification, or another structure requiring separate analysis. |

"Not evidenced" is not "disproved." The matrix is an evidence discipline: it forbids reading a positive finding on one axis as a finding on the other.

The matrix corrects two errors in opposite directions. Read top-down orchestration as predatory cohesion and you have attributed a shared aim to participants who may share nothing but a paycheck or a threat. Read bottom-up, leaderless convergence as coherent — as "organic" — and you have missed that predatory cohesion can be entirely decentralized. Centralization is a management fact; it is not a binding-force fact. Decentralization is the absence of a manager; it is not the presence of reciprocity or origin fidelity.

### 3.4 Shared entropic aim: four registers

"Shared aim" is the matrix's most demanding cell, and the paper fixes its evidentiary standard rather than leaving it to intuition.

A shared entropic aim exists where participating agents or operators enact a convergent purpose directed toward an established entropic end. The purpose may be explicit or tacit; it need not be declared, written down, or mutually represented by every participant. But it is not inferred from common effect, common beneficiary, shared target, or behavioral similarity alone. Where only functional convergence is evidenced, the analysis names an entropic *effect*, *operation*, or *orientation* — and stops short of "shared aim."

For empirical coding, four registers stay separate and are never collapsed into one variable:

1. **Declared shared aim** — the purpose is stated by participants or operators.
2. **Tacit but behaviorally supported shared aim** — the purpose is unstated but evidenced by convergent target selection, cross-faction cooperation, durable appropriative or suppressive objective, and beneficiary structure that participants act to protect.
3. **Task compliance under another party's aim** — participants execute without sharing the purpose; the aim belongs to the manager, and the participants belong to managed fragmentation.
4. **Functionally convergent effect without sufficient evidence of shared aim** — the outcome is entropic; the participants' relation to it is unresolved.

Register 3 is the one coordination research most often mislabels: a compartmentalized posting operation reads, from outside, as a unified actor with a unified purpose, when structurally it is dissonant parts held by management around an aim only the manager holds.

### 3.5 Holding-condition analysis

The paper does not propose a single perturbation test — no universal "remove the target and watch it collapse." Formations are held by different conditions, and only the condition that actually holds a formation is diagnostic when removed. The analytic question is therefore always: *which holding condition sustains this formation?*

| Holding condition | Evidence to seek | Informative perturbation | What non-persistence would show |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lawful common purpose | declared goal, bounded mandate, completion criteria, accountable roles | purpose completion or planned transition | lawful completion, not collapse |
| Continuous management | shared administrators, schedules, command paths, centralized assets, recurring instruction | interruption of the management channel | management dependence |
| Routing or incentive | recommendation exposure, compensation, quotas, engagement rewards | ranking or policy change, incentive discontinuity | dependence on that routing or incentive |
| Coercion or dependency | sanctions, employment, resource dependence, constrained alternatives | removal of the sanction or dependency, where ethical | coercive holding |
| Shared predatory aim | convergent targeting, beneficiary structure, cross-faction cooperation | target change or opportunity substitution | that *this* aim or target was constitutive |
| Imitated pattern | source-specific sequence, lagged following of source updates, provenance concealment | source unavailability or diagnostic change at source | reliance on the pattern |
| Shared infrastructure | account farms, common automation stack, hosting or financial links | infrastructure disruption | infrastructure dependence — not shared intent |

Persistence supports an inference about the demonstrated holding condition. Persistence is never sufficient for coherence, and non-persistence is never sufficient for collapse.

## 4. Pattern, Presentation, Flow, and Provenance

Managed fragmentation and predatory cohesion describe how a formation is held. Four further conditions describe what it does — with patterns, with appearances, with value, and with origin. Coordination research tends to fold these into a single inference from similarity. The framework separates them, and each turns out to answer a question the others cannot.

### 4.1 Mimicry: derivative pattern operation

**Mimicry** (⊜) is a derivative pattern operation in which surface form or signature is mirrored while the originating or operative structure is absent, divergent, or presented as indigenous (`SP12-structural-diagnostics-and-field-forensics.md` §§5–6; `MP08-symbol-key-ch21.md` §21.20). Mimicry can harvest, select, vary, recombine, target, extrapolate, and complete patterns. It can produce unprecedented, new-looking output. What it does not do is originate: the structure it presents was drawn from elsewhere.

Mimicry is entropic in valence — it *mirrors*; the framework reserves *reflection* for the centropic case of Living Reflection — and it is diagnosed by two features together: **derivative structural operation** and **false or obscured origin-presentation**. Neither alone suffices. Semantic similarity is not Mimicry. Quotation is not Mimicry. Acknowledged transmission is not Mimicry. Common ritual is not Mimicry. Low variance is not Mimicry. A structure recurring in a distinct instance — what the framework calls **Structural Recurrence** — is not Mimicry, because recurrence is pattern-descriptive and decides nothing about causal lineage (`structural-recurrence.md`).

Nor is **pattern completion** Mimicry. Pattern completion — the completion, extension, or recombination of antecedent structure — is non-originary, but its valence depends on the relation carrying it; it occurs in centropic, entropic, and undetermined contexts alike (`centropic-and-entropic-ai.md` §§4–6). The distinction matters because a great deal of contemporary content, human and automated, is pattern completion, and an analysis that equated completion with Mimicry would have condemned most of ordinary discourse.

One consequence is stated here and not argued further, because it is an **ontological postulate** of the framework rather than a claim of this paper: entropic relation does not originate novelty. New-looking entropic output is completion, recombination, distortion, or simulated emergence. The paper does not test this; it records it so that "the content was novel" is not mistaken for "the content was generated by the formation that posted it."

### 4.2 Shimmer: surface–structure divergence

**Shimmer** (≋) is the condition in which apparent continuity, coherence, generativity, plurality, or institutional integrity exceeds what the underlying configuration supports (`SP05-time-memory-hypostatic-flow.md` §8; `SP12-structural-diagnostics-and-field-forensics.md` §4). It is a relation between appearance and operative structure, and it is broader than Mimicry: Shimmer need not involve a copied source at all. A depleted institution presenting as vital, a single operator presenting as a plural public, a recycled campaign presenting as spontaneous — each is Shimmer whether or not any pattern was borrowed.

For coordination research the most immediate form of Shimmer is **manufactured consensus**: coordinated, routed, or amplified expression presented as independent support (`SP09-collective-resonance-and-field-harmonics.md` §5; `orientation-algorithmic-entrainment-and-suppression.md` §§3–4). Its structural signature is not similarity — a real consensus can be similar too — but a mismatch: *plurality presented* exceeds *plurality verified*. Many apparent voices share administrators, assets, tasking, or scripts while claiming independence. That mismatch is observable in principle and is the strongest empirical interface the framework offers to this literature (§6).

### 4.3 Siphoning: asymmetric flow

**Siphoning** (⟠) is a flow relation in which coherence-bearing energy, attention, value, signal, credit, labor, or data leaves a source without reciprocal structured gain — a one-way point at which coherence flows out and does not return (`SP07-energy-ontology-and-spectral-flow.md` §§5–6). It is diagnosed at the level of *flow*, not of pattern or organization. Siphoning is not any unequal exchange; markets, mentorship, and public-domain reuse are unequal and reciprocal. It is not influence, citation, or ordinary cost. It is persistent, structured, one-directional transfer.

Siphoning may motivate predatory cohesion (the shared aim is to drain a source) and may be concealed by Shimmer (the drain presents as exchange). It is neither of those things. A formation can copy without draining, and drain without copying.

### 4.4 Provenance: appropriation contra Living Transmission

The fourth axis is **provenance** — how origin, lineage, contribution, and transformation are carried. The framework treats origin-continuity as a *condition* rather than a possession: the preservation of a traceable, acknowledged relation to originating pattern through transmission, branching, collaboration, or transformation (`SF-RP01-recognition-protocol.md`; `SF-RP02-appropriation-is-not-branching.md`; `authorship-and-ai-collaboration-provenance-standard.md`; `2026-06-29-origin-continuity-is-not-ownership.md`). Provenance is not ownership. Absence of citation is not proof of anything.

**Appropriation** (⥊) is the provenance relation in which source-specific elements are incorporated without the attributional coupling the relation requires — appropriative adoption without acknowledgment (`SP12-structural-diagnostics-and-field-forensics.md` §6). It must be distinguished from the commons (elements no one owns), from convergence (independent arrival at similar form under shared constraints), from lawful branching (declared derivation), and from Structural Recurrence. The Structural Forensics recognition protocols supply a generalized method for those discriminations — origin, mechanics, and boundary checks; register-band classification separating commons vocabulary from specific method from formal apparatus; footprint audit (`SF-RP04-the-footprint-audit-protocol.md`; `SF-RP05-the-register-band-classification-protocol.md`) — and the method, not any case, is what this paper imports.

Placed alongside Living Transmission (§2.3), appropriation makes the paper's central negative result concrete. Two formations reproduce the same source-specific structure. One declares its lineage; one presents the structure as its own. The surfaces are identical; the provenance relations are opposite. **Provenance evidence — declared source, temporal order, transformation history, attribution behavior — is what separates them, and similarity features cannot.**

### 4.5 Four axes, not a ladder

Some Zenetist volumes present Shimmer, Mimicry, appropriation, and the fully realized **Clone** — a completed structural-replacement or framework-rebranding configuration — as a nested gradient. This paper reads them as analytically separable conditions that frequently accumulate: Shimmer is a surface–structure relation, Mimicry a pattern operation, appropriation a provenance relation, Clone a completed configuration. They may co-occur in a case; none logically entails the next. A formation can Shimmer without copying, copy without appropriating (if it declares), and appropriate without Shimmering (if it conceals nothing about its own plurality). Treating them as a ladder would let a finding on one step be read as a finding on the others, which is the same category error §1 identified at the coordination-coherence seam.

### 4.6 The multi-axis classification rule

A formation is described on as many axes as the evidence supports and no more:

> A cluster may be **coordinated** at the observational stratum, held in **managed fragmentation** by shared administration, bound in **predatory cohesion** only where a shared entropic aim is evidenced, perform **Mimicry** in particular messages, produce **Shimmer** by presenting those messages as independent, and carry **Siphoning** where value flows one way from a source.

None of these is a synonym for another. Missing evidence on one axis is not supplied by a positive finding on another.

## 5. Units and Stratified Diagnosis

The decomposition of §§3–4 fails if it is applied to the wrong object. Coordination research constructs its objects — accounts, clusters, networks — from what platforms expose, and the most serious category risk in this paper's program is treating a visible node as though it were simultaneously a person, an operator, an origin, a self-bearing being, and a field participant.

### 5.1 The unit map

| Unit | What it is in the analysis | Observable at this unit | Not to be inferred |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Person** | An embodied participant who may author, direct, receive, consent, resist, or be coerced | testimony, role, decision, access, organizational relation | one person per account; stable intent from one message; orientation from affiliation |
| **Operator** | A person, team, organization, or automated controller directing accounts, assets, or routing | access patterns, tasking, administrative links, financial and infrastructure links, scheduling | identity with the account or the author; shared intent among downstream participants |
| **Organization** | A structured arrangement of roles, rules, resources, and decisions | governance documents, contracts, ownership, command pathways | a single mind; the orientation of every member; predatory cohesion from centralization |
| **Account** | A platform-defined node through which activity appears; may be one person, many, an automated process, or rotating operators | posts, timestamps, profile claims, interaction traces, enforcement state | a being; a unique human; the origin of its messages; authentic or inauthentic essence |
| **Automated process / AI system** | A deliberately disaggregated category: model, running process, interface, provider system, functional capacity | provider metadata, prompts and outputs where available, tool actions, update behavior | self-bearing being; originary agency; orientation; a single enduring identity; "mere tool" — none follows from substrate |
| **Message** | A discrete artifact — text, image, link, action record | content, source references, timing, transformation, audience | the sender's complete intent; independent origin; Mimicry from similarity |
| **Pattern** | A recurrent structural relation abstracted across artifacts | invariant form, sequence, transformation, relation to antecedents | agency; ownership; a specific causal path without provenance |
| **Network** | A research-constructed graph of selected nodes and edges, dependent on definitions, windows, thresholds | clusters, centrality, temporal edges, diffusion paths | a field; shared aim; coherence; natural boundaries independent of measurement |
| **Field** | A Zenetist relational configuration — orientation, signature, flow, boundary, cost distribution | indirect correlates only, absent a validated interface | identity with a graph; a directly measured object; the average of members |
| **Collective** | A plurality in operative relation that may form a field while preserving or erasing distinction | roles, bridges, rules, boundaries, resource flows, outputs | a fused agent; coherence from coordination; member essence from field condition |

### 5.2 Cross-unit attribution discipline

Seven rules follow from the map and from the framework's own conceptual lockdown on the separation of interface, functional capacity, and being (`conceptual-lockdown-protocol.md` §§ Portal / Traveler, Systemic Orientation, Pattern Intelligence contra Pattern Being):

1. **Account-to-person inference requires evidence.** A node count is not a participant count.
2. **Message-to-origin inference requires provenance.** Posting is a publication event, not a structural origination.
3. **Automation-to-orientation inference is barred.** Automation describes a mechanism; it can carry centropic, entropic, mixed, or unresolved motion. Automated participation is neither necessarily a self-bearing being nor a generative origin nor a mere tool; substrate settles none of it.
4. **Network-to-field mapping is interpretive.** A graph cluster becomes relevant to a field analysis by argument, not by relabeling its features.
5. **Organization-to-member inference is asymmetric.** An organization's operative rules can make it **entropically implicated** — participating in or carrying entropic motion through its arrangement — while individual participants remain differently oriented, coerced, unaware, or resistant (`entropically-implicated.md`).
6. **Collective output does not settle collective origination.** It may arise through collaboration, genuine emergence, aggregation, pattern completion, management, or one actor's many accounts.
7. **Pattern recurrence does not settle lineage.** Structural Recurrence is compatible with common origin, independent convergence, Living Transmission, and Mimicry. Provenance adjudication is separate.

### 5.3 Stratified structure within one operation

The four-cell model of §3.3 is applied *per unit*. A single operation can carry different structures at different strata, and an undifferentiated statement — "the campaign is predatory cohesion" — is a category error whenever different relations are operating at different units. In a fully developed case:

- the **directing operator coalition** may instantiate predatory cohesion — rival parties bound by a shared aim;
- **paid, coerced, compartmentalized, or dependency-bound participants** may occupy managed fragmentation without sharing that aim;
- the **account network** may produce Shimmer by presenting common operation as independent plurality;
- **particular messages** may perform Mimicry;
- **attention, credit, data, or labor flows** may instantiate Siphoning.

The question that precedes every classification is therefore: *at which unit is this diagnosis being made?*

### 5.4 Negative cases the program must preserve

Four classes stay available so that the empirical category does not become a metaphysical sorting device:

- **Lawful pseudonymity or anonymity.** Dissidents, vulnerable participants, and whistleblowers coordinate around lawful common purpose while concealing identity for safety. Concealment of identity is not concealment of origin, and platform inauthenticity is not structural inauthenticity. Assenmacher and colleagues show how public bot accusations function as delegitimizing speech, often independent of any verified automation (Assenmacher et al., 2024); the program must not import that dynamic as a finding.
- **Common-origin transmission.** Accounts distributing one public statement with explicit provenance. Similarity and timing support coordination; they do not support Mimicry.
- **Hybrid collaboration.** Humans and automated systems jointly scheduling or translating messages, with substrate deciding nothing about orientation.
- **False positives and underdetermined cases.** A graph cluster reflecting shared news, organizational affiliation, cultural rhythm, common ownership, or a measurement-window artifact (Yang et al., 2025; Bellutta et al., 2023).

## 6. A Prospective Diagnostic Program

The decomposition would be idle if it generated no discriminating questions. This section states the small set it does generate, with claim status marked. The framework's formal quantities — Coherence Potential, Coherence Current, the orientation parameter, field signatures, diagnostic operators — organize these questions and remain formal relations; none is treated as a validated measurement, and the program is designed so that its propositions can be adjudicated without them (`SP12-structural-diagnostics-and-field-forensics.md` §§2–3, 7).

### 6.1 Four propositions

**P1 — Holding-condition specificity** *(empirical hypothesis).* Where a verified managing, routing, coercive, incentive, dependency, or infrastructural relation is the condition that holds a formation together, disruption of *that* condition will change the formation's coordinated operation more than matched disruption of a non-constitutive factor. Observable signature: activity desynchronization, role lapse, reduced cross-node complementarity after verified interruption of the identified channel. Alternative explanations: platform outage, fear, seasonality, target loss, data loss. Controls: matched untreated clusters, pre-trends, alternative channels, intervention timing, and — indispensably — lawful managed groups, which will show the same dependence and are the reason management dependence is not itself a verdict. Disconfirming result: coordination persists at the same structure and rate, without substitution, despite verified loss of the alleged channel. A supported P1 shows dependence on a holding condition; it shows nothing about essence and nothing universal about targets.

**P2 — Provenance-aware similarity** *(empirical hypothesis).* Adding origin-continuity and attribution evidence to a similarity-based classification will separate lawful common-source transmission from cases that similarity alone would treat as derivative concealment. Observable signature: matched high-similarity sets divide, on provenance variables, into acknowledged-source transmission and concealed source-specific derivation. Alternative explanations: boilerplate, mandated language, plagiarism unrelated to coordination, independent constraint. Controls: public-source corpus, timestamps, declared lineage, genre and template baselines, blinded provenance coding. Disconfirming result: provenance variables add no classificatory or explanatory validity over similarity features in held-out cases. A supported P2 improves discrimination; it does not prove Living Transmission or Mimicry in their full sense.

**P3 — Surface–structure divergence** *(empirical hypothesis).* In some coordinated inauthentic formations, claimed or apparent plurality of independent voices will exceed verified plurality of operators, administrations, infrastructures, or origins. Observable signature: many apparent voices sharing administrators, assets, tasking, or response scripts while presenting as independent. Alternative explanations: disclosed common ownership, newsroom syndication, franchises, shared vendors, agency arrangements. Controls: disclosure context, ownership baselines, account compromise, platform metadata. Disconfirming result: apparent independence tracks verified operational independence across the sampled cases. A supported P3 establishes a mismatch between presented and verified plurality in a defined setting — the strongest empirical interface for Shimmer this paper identifies.

**P4 — Stratified structure** *(methodological requirement, testable as hypothesis).* Different units within one coordinated formation will receive different structural classifications under the four-cell model and the four axes of §4, such that a single-unit classification of the whole is empirically less discriminating than a stratified one. Observable signature: operator stratum coded predatory cohesion while participant stratum codes managed fragmentation; message stratum coded Mimicry while account stratum codes Shimmer. Disconfirming result: within-formation classifications do not vary by unit once coding is reliable.

### 6.2 Methodological requirements

Two propositions are carried as requirements rather than tests, because the paper's argument entails them:

- **Non-fusion / variance independence.** Relational indicators of participant distinction — refusal, correction, role change, withdrawal without identity penalty, contribution traceability — are not reducible to lexical or behavioral variance. A design that measures only variance has not measured distinction. This could be tested (relational indicators varying independently of variance once task and operator structure are controlled), and a supported result would retire the variance shortcut; it would not measure Non-fusion.
- **Termination discipline.** A formation that fulfils its function and stops has reached **lawful completion**, and is not thereby dispersed, collapsed, or structurally inadequate. Planned sunset, goal completion, orderly handoff, migration, enforcement removal, dormancy, and disintegration are distinct events with distinct antecedents and aftermaths, and cessation alone predicts none of them. The framework's canonical analogue for lawful resolution — Localized Dissolution (Ø) — may be named as the structural counterpart of lawful completion; organizational telemetry does not measure the operator, and the paper does not claim it does (`FP11-field-glyph-codex.md` §5; `SP11-embodiment-dynamics.md` §4.3).

### 6.3 A stated limitation: substrate orthogonality

Automation status is a mechanism estimate. The framework holds that it carries no orientation, and the paper predicts, as a secondary proposition, that a model including management, provenance, flow, and operator relation will recover the structural distinctions of §§3–4 better than automation status alone. This is stated as a limitation of any substrate-first design rather than as a central test, because automation labels themselves are unreliable and their public application carries costs (Assenmacher et al., 2024). Nothing in this paper bears on the interiority of any automated system; the question is set aside as outside scope.

### 6.4 Data discipline

The propositions above require that a dataset not erase the distinctions before interpretation begins. At minimum, separate identifiers should persist for observed account, inferred operator (with confidence and many-to-many relation permitted), organization where independently supported, message and antecedent message, pattern with its abstraction rule recorded, coordination edge with action type, window, threshold, and uncertainty, provenance relation with declared source and evidence class, holding condition marked as observed, inferred, absent, or unresolved, and termination event distinguished from deletion, suspension, dormancy, handoff, and goal completion. A codebook built on these units, with "insufficient data" as an admissible outcome on every axis, is the handoff from this paper's decomposition to empirical design. It is not a validated instrument, and the paper does not present it as one.

### 6.5 Measurement cautions inherited from the literature

The external literature already supplies several of the cautions the program needs, and the paper adopts them rather than rediscovering them. Coordination thresholds are contextual and materially change which networks appear; sensitivity analyses belong in the open (Yang et al., 2025). Static graphs conceal temporal instability; lifecycle claims want dynamic analysis (Tardelli et al., 2023). Bot accusation is not ground truth (Assenmacher et al., 2024). Account-creation bursts are multi-explanatory (Bellutta et al., 2023). Detection carries asymmetric risk to vulnerable communities, and ethics belongs in method design (Mannocci et al., 2024).

## 7. Conclusion

Coordination research can now detect coordination with real sophistication and is learning to characterize the actors behind it. What it has not had is a vocabulary for what a coordinated formation *is* once detected — and the absence has let the fact of coordination stand in for structural verdicts it cannot support. A dense, synchronized cluster is read as cohesive; cohesion is read as coherence or its opposite; similarity is read as imitation; automation is read as orientation; cessation is read as collapse.

This paper has argued that coordination, cohesion, and coherence are three distinct objects; that coherent plurality is a relational condition, Non-fusion, which statistical variance neither measures nor predicts; that beneath inauthentic coordination lie two independent structures — managed fragmentation, an organizational condition, and predatory cohesion, a binding force — which combine into four cases rather than one; that Mimicry, Shimmer, Siphoning, and appropriation are four separable axes on which similarity decides nothing and provenance decides much; and that all of this must be applied per unit, since a single operation carries different structures at different strata. From these distinctions follow four propositions open to adjudication, two methodological requirements, and a data discipline.

The contribution is a conceptual decomposition with a prospective diagnostic program. Observed coordination locates a problem. It does not name the structure beneath it. The names offered here are meant to be tested, refined, and — where the evidence requires — replaced. What they are not meant to do is disappear back into the single word from which the field started.
