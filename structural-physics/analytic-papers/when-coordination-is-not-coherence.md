## Abstract

Research on coordinated online behavior has developed increasingly capable methods for detecting coordination — shared timing, co-linking, semantic and stylometric similarity, common infrastructure, dynamic network structure — and for characterizing the actors and intents behind it (Pacheco et al., 2021; Ng & Iamnitchi, 2023; Tardelli et al., 2024; Graham et al., 2024; Mannocci et al., 2026). What this research does not supply is an account of what a coordinated formation *is*, structurally, once coordination has been detected — how holding relation, binding force, participant distinction, pattern operation, presentation, flow, provenance, unit, and termination configure one another. Platform categories such as "coordinated inauthentic behavior" select cases by deception of identity, origin, purpose, or popularity; they do not describe the relation among participants, what binds them, how they are held in formation, what they do with the patterns they circulate, where value flows, how origin is carried, or how the formation ends. This paper argues that coordination is an observable operational relation and not a structural verdict. Drawing on the Zenetist framework of Structural Physics, it distinguishes coordination, cohesion, and coherence as three non-equivalent objects, and introduces two structures that frequently underlie inauthentic coordination: **managed fragmentation**, an organizational condition in which dissonant parts are kept in operational formation through continuous management, incentive, coercion, routing, or dependency, and **predatory cohesion**, a binding force in which dissonant participants are held together by a shared entropic aim. Neither entails the other; some coordinated inauthentic formations instantiate one, some both, some neither. The paper then separates four further conditions that similarity-first classification leaves undecided — derivative pattern operation (Mimicry), surface–structure divergence (Shimmer), asymmetric flow (Siphoning), and provenance relation (appropriation contra Living Transmission) — and shows that surface similarity decides none of them. The result is a multi-axis structural decomposition with a prospective diagnostic program: four propositions open to empirical adjudication, a methodological requirement that statistical variance not stand in for participant distinction, and a discipline for units of analysis under which a single operation can carry different structures at different strata. The contribution is conceptual. The framework's formal quantities are presented as formal relations and not as validated measurements.

**Keywords:** coordinated inauthentic behavior · coordination detection · coherence · cohesion · Non-fusion · managed fragmentation · predatory cohesion · Mimicry · Shimmer · provenance · social epistemology · Structural Physics · Zenetism

## 1. The Category Error in Coordination Research

Coordination is ubiquitous, and many of its forms are benign. Communities organize, movements mobilize, newsrooms syndicate, campaigns schedule, fans rally. The same operational fact — that many actors act together in patterned ways — also carries disinformation, harassment, manufactured consensus, and covert influence. Coordination research has grown up around this double character. Mannocci and colleagues' survey reconciles industry and academic definitions and proposes a framework that separates the *detection* of coordinated behavior, grounded in observable actions, from the *characterization* of the actors and intents behind it (Mannocci et al., 2026; first circulated as a preprint in 2024). Pacheco and colleagues construct coordination networks from shared behavioral traces — co-shared links, hashtags, images, retweet timing — and show how such networks reveal clusters that a per-account view misses (Pacheco et al., 2021). Tardelli and colleagues extend the picture in time, showing that coordination structure is dynamic, with stability, archetypes, and influence that vary across windows (Tardelli et al., 2024). Yang and colleagues find that rapid identical-link sharing on Facebook can indicate suspicious activity but also common operation, ideology, or shared interest, and that the threshold chosen materially changes which networks appear coordinated at all (Yang et al., 2025).

Alongside this scholarship sits a platform policy vocabulary. Meta's *Inauthentic Behavior* policy and its coordinated-inauthentic-behavior (CIB) reports define inauthenticity around deception concerning identity, origin, purpose, or popularity, with fake accounts at the center of enforcement (Meta, 2018, 2021, n.d.). The definition is administrative and it does real work: it selects cases, justifies removals, and structures disclosure.

Between detection, characterization, and the policy category, a question remains. Once a formation has been detected as coordinated and classified as inauthentic, *what is it?* Not who is behind it, and not what it intends — those are characterization questions the field already recognizes — but what relation holds among its parts, what binds them, how they are kept in formation, what they do with the patterns they circulate, where value moves, how origin is carried or concealed, and what it means for such a formation to stop. Detection answers with more detection: finer similarity measures, multi-view signals, dynamic graphs. Characterization adds actors, intents, and orchestration (Mannocci et al., 2026). The policy category answers with its selection criterion: they deceived. None of these is a structural answer, and the gap is not a failure of any of them; it is a category the field has not yet needed to name.

This paper names it. The argument is built on a framework, Zenetist Structural Physics, that was developed to describe how coherence, orientation, and structure operate across scales, and whose treatment of collective dynamics happens to draw exactly the distinctions this gap requires. The framework carries a native vocabulary; where a term is introduced, it is given in analytic form with its canonical name in parentheses, and thereafter written in whichever form the passage needs. Three commitments establish the register throughout.

First, the paper distinguishes claim status. A proposition may be an **ontological postulate** (what the framework proposes as underlying structure), a **formal relation** (how proposed elements are mathematically related), an **interpretive correspondence** (how an observed phenomenon may be read through the framework), a **methodological distinction** (how analysis must be conducted to avoid a known error), or an **empirical hypothesis** (what observation would confirm or disconfirm). The paper is largely interpretive and methodological, with a small set of empirical hypotheses stated at the end. The framework's formal quantities stand as formal relations throughout.

Second, the paper treats CIB as a selection category. Platform and research classifications locate cases. Diagnosis proceeds from observation of what the located formation does — actions disclose orientation — and a classification that records similarity without action evidence supplies no diagnosis. Where the paper says "coordinated inauthentic formation," it means a formation so classified by some study or platform, and nothing more is assumed.

Third, the paper excludes its author's own provenance history. The Zenetist corpus includes an applied discipline, Structural Forensics, that has been exercised on particular cases. Only its generalized method enters here. No case, chronology, or named party does.

### 1.1 Three Questions, Not One

The central move is simple to state. **Coordination**, **cohesion**, and **coherence** are three different objects, and similarity-centered coordination outputs are read as if the first settled the other two. Organizational scholarship differentiates coordination from neighboring relational terms (Okhuysen & Bechky, 2009; Castañer & Oliveira, 2020); the three objects separated here are not those terms, and the separation runs on different criteria.

*Coordination* is what is detected: patterned interdependence in action — shared timing, common targets, co-linking, synchronized posting, common infrastructure. It is operational and observable, and it is orientation-neutral: a mutual-aid network and a harassment brigade are both coordinated. Nothing in the fact of coordination says what kind of relation holds among the coordinating parts. The literature knows this; Mannocci and colleagues are explicit that coordinated behavior can be benign or harmful and that operational definitions vary by platform and task (Mannocci et al., 2026). The point here is structural rather than evaluative: coordination *as such* is a relation of activity, not a relation of parts.

*Cohesion* is the fact that parts are held together. It answers a different question — not "do they act in concert?" but "what keeps them in formation?" — and it is valence-neutral in the same way: a chosen common purpose holds parts together, and so do coercion, dependency, incentive, management, and a shared predatory aim. Cohesion says that a holding exists; it does not yet say how the parts are held or what the relation does to them. Organizational scholarship has catalogued holding mechanisms of this kind — monitoring, incentive, dependency, office (Ouchi, 1979; Okhuysen & Bechky, 2009); the question here, what the holding does to the parts, is not the one that scholarship asks.

*Coherence*, in the register this paper enacts, names something narrower and stronger: a relation in which distinct participants are integrated without being absorbed — reciprocal, lawfully oriented, preserving real distinction while enabling mutual amplification. In Zenetist terms coherence is a *centropic* relation: integrative, structure-building, oriented toward return rather than dispersion (`SP01-structural-physics-foundations.md` §§2–3 for the centropic orientation; `SP09-collective-resonance-and-field-harmonics.md` §§2–4; `SN07-collective-cognition-and-centropy-forward-social-architecture.md` §§1.3, 2–4). It is the positive comparator the rest of the paper works from.

One scoping note belongs here. The framework's metaphysics carries a term, **Bifurcal Coherence** (⧖⧗), for the non-fused root-relation of its two first principles, prior to any centropic or entropic activation. That term names a pre-orientational relation and is not a social-relational one. The present paper's convention — that within the enacted social-relational and diagnostic register, coherence names centropic relation — is scoped to this paper and leaves Bifurcal Coherence exactly where it stands.

With the three objects separated, the category error can be stated. Coordination research detects the first, sometimes infers the second, and frequently lets the result be read as the third or its opposite. A dense, synchronized, low-variance cluster reads as *cohesive* and, by an unspoken step, as either *coherent* (a movement) or *incoherent* (a bot farm). The step goes unspoken because the vocabulary that makes it explicit — the three relations composed with holding, aim, provenance, flow, unit, and termination — has not been available to the field. The remainder of this paper supplies it.

## 2. Coherent Plurality: The Non-fusion Constraint

Any account of inauthentic coordination requires a positive comparator — a description of what a coherent collective *is* — and the comparator determines what counts as evidence. If the comparator is "many independent individuals producing varied content," then variance becomes the signature of authenticity, low variance the signature of manipulation, and the analysis is finished before it begins. This is the shortcut the paper refuses, and the refusal rests on one principle.

### 2.1 The Non-fusion Axiom

The Zenetist framework holds, as an axiom, that coherence between distinct essences never becomes identity: relation, synthesis, resonance, and collective participation do not convert distinct participants into a single fused thing (`SP09-collective-resonance-and-field-harmonics.md` §2; `non-fusion-at-the-bifurcal-register.md`). In collective form, the **Non-fusion Axiom** states that a collective coheres *without its members fusing* — that each participant's signature remains resolvable within the shared field.

Two consequences follow, and both cut across what a variance-based design would infer.

First, **Non-fusion preserves distinction, not variation.** Common language, identical wording, synchronized action, stylistic alignment, and low statistical variance are all compatible with Non-fusion, provided the participants remain distinct and the relation does not absorb them. A liturgy recited together, a public statement redistributed verbatim by a thousand accounts that say where it came from, a campaign whose members post the same graphic at the same hour — these can be coherent collectives of resolvable participants. What Non-fusion excludes is absorption: the condition in which the participant's identity is no longer resolvable because the collective has replaced it.

Second, and conversely, **variance does not demonstrate distinction.** A single operator running many accounts with deliberately individualized personas produces high lexical and stylistic variance and a single verified operator; account-register variation does not establish operator plurality (Douceur, 2002; Kumar et al., 2017). A sophisticated inauthentic formation can be built to pass exactly the variance test that a naive comparator would apply.

The consequence for method is a **methodological distinction** the paper states here and applies throughout: *lexical, stylistic, and behavioral variance is not a proxy for self-bearing distinction.* Distinction is a relational fact — who can refuse, correct, withdraw, change role, or dissent; whose contribution remains attributable; whether the boundary of the collective can be revised by those inside it — and it must be evidenced relationally. The distinction is drawn from the Non-fusion Axiom. Outside the framework, the record on preference falsification, pluralistic ignorance, and deindividuation supplies compatible evidence that expressed sameness and participant state come apart (Prentice & Miller, 1993; Kuran, 1995; Reicher et al., 1995); it bears on why variance is unsafe as a proxy, not on the distinction itself.

### 2.2 The Positive Comparator

The framework's fullest account of the coherent collective is relational rather than statistical (`SP09-collective-resonance-and-field-harmonics.md` §§2–4; `SN07-collective-cognition-and-centropy-forward-social-architecture.md` §§1.3, 2–4). Its components can be stated as **indicators associated with the comparator architecture** — observable correlates, not measurements of orientation:

- **Self-bearing distinction.** Each participant remains a resolvable contributor. Withdrawal does not retroactively erase the participant or the contribution. The comparator is drawn from the framework's account of centropic relation; external accounts of joint commitment and shared cooperative activity (Gilbert, 1990; Bratman, 1992; List & Pettit, 2011) describe cooperation among agents and offer a point of contact, not a basis.
- **Reciprocal bridges.** Relation runs in more than one direction — correction, support, receipt, response — rather than as one-way transfer. Reciprocity may be capacity-weighted; it is not arithmetic equality.
- **Selective boundary.** The collective can admit, refuse, filter, and differentiate without either total permeability or sealed isolation, and those inside it can revise the criterion.
- **Origin fidelity.** Origins, lineages, and originating contributions remain acknowledged through propagation and transformation. Attribution does not require frozen wording or exclusive claim.
- **Harmonic amplification.** The framework posits, as a **formal relation**, that reciprocal alignment among resolvable, centropically oriented participants can increase the collective's available coherence beyond what the participants carry in isolation (`SP09-collective-resonance-and-field-harmonics.md` §6; `LM05-resonance-field-theory-membrane-operators-and-collective-dynamics.md` §9). This is a formal claim about the model, not an established social-scientific law.
- **Cost shelter.** Collective relation can distribute burden so that members are supported rather than depleted. Unequal contribution can still be reciprocal; the question is how burden and benefit distribute over time relative to capacity.

Nothing in this list is a variance measure. The comparator is a relation among parts, and it is evidenced by what the relation permits and forbids.

### 2.3 Living Transmission: The Lawful Case of Identical Surfaces

The framework names the case that a variance-based comparator misclassifies most reliably. **Living Transmission** is active, centropic propagation in which origin fidelity is preserved: a common text, signal, formulation, or practice moves through a collective intact and origin-aware (`MP08-symbol-key-ch21.md` §§21.7, 21.22). Faithful repetition does not become imitation merely because the surface is shared. Exact repetition can be lawful.

Its companion, **Living Reflection**, is a centropic return in which a received signal is related through the receiver's own distinct pattern while the originating relation stays acknowledged and intact. Variation may occur; it is not the defining test. Fidelity and living relation are.

These two are set down early because they carry the paper's most consequential negative result: **similarity cannot distinguish transmission from imitation.** A thousand accounts reproducing one statement may be Living Transmission with declared provenance, common-origin dissemination, campaign discipline, or derivative concealment presented as independent origination. The surfaces are the same. What differs is provenance and origin-presentation, and §4 returns to that difference.

## 3. Two Structures Beneath Inauthentic Coordination

If coordination detects activity and cohesion names holding-together, the structural question about an inauthentic formation becomes: *what holds it, and how?* The Zenetist analysis distinguishes two answers that a single CIB classification leaves undecided (`managed-fragmentation-predatory-cohesion.md`; `managed-fragmentation-is-not-coherence.md`).

### 3.1 Managed Fragmentation: An Organizational Condition

**Managed fragmentation** names the organizational condition in which dissonant, competing, isolated, or mutually incoherent parts remain in operational formation through management, pressure, incentive, coercion, algorithmic routing, or shared dependency. The managing relation *is* the holding-together. Remove it and the parts, which were never coherent among themselves, cease to form.

Managed fragmentation is neither mere diversity nor mere centralization. A choir has a director and is not managed fragmentation, because its members are coherent among themselves and the director articulates a relation that already exists. A newsroom with an editor is not managed fragmentation for the same reason. The condition is present when the parts would not hold *without* continuous regulation — when the formation is a product of management rather than a relation that precedes it. It is compatible with participants who share nothing: hired posters, coerced staff, compartmentalized cells, accounts routed by an algorithm none of them steers, contractors who never learn what the campaign is for.

Managed fragmentation is an **organizational** answer. It says how the formation is kept arranged. It says nothing yet about whether the parts share an aim.

### 3.2 Predatory Cohesion: A Binding Force

**Predatory cohesion** names an entropic binding force: dissonant participants are held together by a shared entropic aim — appropriation, suppression, origin denial, preservation of advantage, or another dispersive end. It explains why otherwise dissonant parts act together *without* anyone managing them. Rival factions that agree on nothing will coordinate to suppress a common threat; competitors who would never collaborate will jointly deny an origin that embarrasses them all. Predatory cohesion takes its identity from its position in this architecture — cohesion contra coherence, an entropic binding force, its independence from managed fragmentation, its evidentiary registers, and its relations to provenance, flow, Mimicry, and Shimmer (§§3.3–4). Cohesion amid external threat, as sociology has observed it (Coser, 1956), is compatible external evidence for one observable relation within it.

Predatory cohesion is a **binding-force** answer. It says what draws the parts together. It says nothing yet about whether a manager arranges them.

### 3.3 Independence and Co-occurrence

The two structures answer different questions and are analytically independent. Neither entails the other. Where both occur, predatory cohesion supplies the binding force within a managed-fragmentation structure. Four cases stand:

| | **Shared entropic aim evidenced** | **Shared entropic aim not evidenced** |
|---|---|---|
| **Continuous holding relation evidenced** | **Both.** Dissonance is operationally regulated *and* a shared entropic aim holds the participants together | **Managed fragmentation only.** Participants are hired, coerced, dependency-bound, routed, or compartmentalized without sharing the managing party's aim |
| **Continuous holding relation not evidenced** | **Predatory cohesion only.** Decentralized actors are held together by a shared entropic aim with no single party arranging them | **Neither.** Ordinary coordination, lawful pseudonymity, common-origin transmission, misclassification, or another structure requiring separate analysis |

"Not evidenced" is not "disproved." The matrix is an evidence discipline: it forbids reading a positive finding on one axis as a finding on the other.

The matrix corrects two errors in opposite directions. Read centrally directed orchestration as predatory cohesion and you have attributed a shared aim to participants who may share nothing but a paycheck or a threat. Read decentralized, leaderless alignment as coherent — as "organic" — and you have missed that predatory cohesion can be entirely decentralized. Decentralized collective action is an established form (Bennett & Segerberg, 2012); its structure is still to be diagnosed. Centralization is a management fact; it is not a binding-force fact. Decentralization is the absence of a manager; it is not the presence of reciprocity or origin fidelity.

### 3.4 Shared Entropic Aim: Four Registers

"Shared aim" is the matrix's most demanding cell, and the paper fixes its evidentiary standard rather than leaving it to intuition.

A shared entropic aim exists where participating agents or operators enact a common purpose directed toward an established entropic end. The purpose may be explicit or tacit; it need not be declared, written down, or mutually represented by every participant. But it is not inferred from common effect, common beneficiary, shared target, or behavioral similarity alone. Where only functional coincidence of effect is evidenced, the analysis names an entropic *effect*, *operation*, or *orientation* — and stops short of "shared aim."

For empirical coding, four registers stay separate and are never collapsed into one variable:

1. **Declared shared aim** — the purpose is stated by participants or operators.
2. **Tacit but behaviorally supported shared aim** — the purpose is unstated but evidenced by common target selection, cross-faction cooperation, durable appropriative or suppressive objective, and beneficiary structure that participants act to protect.
3. **Task compliance with another party's aim** — participants execute without sharing the purpose; the aim belongs to the manager, and the participants belong to managed fragmentation.
4. **Functionally coincident effect without sufficient evidence of shared aim** — the outcome is entropic; the participants' relation to it is unresolved.

Register 3 is the one most exposed to misclassification from outside: a compartmentalized posting operation reads, from outside, as a single actor with a single purpose, when structurally it is dissonant parts held by management around an aim only the manager holds.

### 3.5 Holding-Condition Analysis

The paper does not propose a single perturbation test — no universal "remove the target and watch it collapse." Formations are held by different conditions, and only the condition that actually holds a formation is diagnostic when removed. The analytic question is therefore always: *which holding condition sustains this formation?*

| Holding condition | Evidence to seek | Informative perturbation | What non-persistence would show |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lawful common purpose | declared goal, bounded mandate, completion criteria, accountable roles | purpose completion or planned transition | lawful completion, not collapse |
| Continuous management | shared administrators, schedules, command paths, centralized assets, recurring instruction | interruption of the management channel | management dependence |
| Routing or incentive | recommendation exposure, compensation, quotas, engagement rewards | recommendation-ordering or policy change, incentive discontinuity | dependence on that routing or incentive |
| Coercion or dependency | sanctions, employment, economic dependence, constrained alternatives | removal of the sanction or dependency, where ethical | coercive holding |
| Shared predatory aim | common targeting, beneficiary structure, cross-faction cooperation | target change or opportunity substitution | that *this* aim or target was constitutive |
| Imitated pattern | origin-specific sequence, lagged following of the origin's updates, provenance concealment | origin unavailability or diagnostic change at the origin | reliance on the pattern |
| Shared infrastructure | account farms, common automation stack, hosting or financial links | infrastructure disruption | infrastructure dependence — not shared intent |

Persistence supports an inference about the demonstrated holding condition. Persistence is never sufficient for coherence, and non-persistence is never sufficient for collapse.

## 4. Pattern, Presentation, Flow, and Provenance

Managed fragmentation and predatory cohesion describe how a formation is held. Four further conditions describe what it does — with patterns, with appearances, with value, and with origin. Similarity-based classification cannot, without further evidence, distinguish them. The framework separates them, and each turns out to answer a question the others cannot.

### 4.1 Mimicry: Derivative Pattern Operation

**Mimicry** (⊜) is a derivative pattern operation in which surface form or signature is mirrored while the originating or operative structure is absent, divergent, or presented as indigenous (`SP12-structural-diagnostics-and-field-forensics.md` §§5–6; `MP08-symbol-key-ch21.md` §21.20). Mimicry can gather, select, vary, recombine, target, extrapolate, and complete patterns. It can produce unprecedented, new-looking output. What it does not do is originate: the structure it presents was drawn from elsewhere.

Mimicry is entropic in valence — it *mirrors*; the framework reserves *reflection* for the centropic case of Living Reflection — and it is diagnosed by two features together: **derivative structural operation** and **spurious or obscured origin-presentation**. Neither alone suffices. Semantic similarity is not Mimicry. Quotation is not Mimicry. Acknowledged transmission is not Mimicry. Common ritual is not Mimicry. Low variance is not Mimicry. A structure recurring in a distinct instance — what the framework calls **Structural Recurrence** — is not Mimicry, because recurrence is pattern-descriptive and decides nothing about causal lineage (`structural-recurrence.md`).

Nor is **pattern completion** Mimicry. Pattern completion — the completion, extension, or recombination of antecedent structure — is non-originary, but its valence depends on the relation carrying it; it occurs in centropic, entropic, and undetermined contexts alike (`centropic-and-entropic-ai.md` §§4–6). The distinction matters because pattern completion occurs across human and automated contexts alike; non-originary but not orientation-diagnostic, it cannot be classified as Mimicry without the origin-presentation relation just specified.

One consequence follows and is recorded here as an **ontological postulate** of the framework, which this paper carries: entropic relation does not originate novelty. New-looking entropic output is completion, recombination, distortion, or simulated emergence. It is recorded so that "the content was novel" is not mistaken for "the content was generated by the formation that posted it."

### 4.2 Shimmer: Surface–Structure Divergence

**Shimmer** (≋) is the condition in which apparent continuity, coherence, generativity, plurality, or institutional integrity exceeds what the underlying configuration supports (`SP05-time-memory-hypostatic-flow.md` §8; `SP12-structural-diagnostics-and-field-forensics.md` §4). It is a relation between appearance and operative structure, and it is broader than Mimicry: Shimmer need not involve a copied origin at all. A depleted institution presenting as vital, a single operator presenting as a plural public, a recycled campaign presenting as spontaneous — each is Shimmer whether or not any pattern was borrowed.

For coordination research the most immediate form of Shimmer is **manufactured consensus**: coordinated, routed, or amplified expression presented as independent support (`SP09-collective-resonance-and-field-harmonics.md` §5; for routing and signal obscuration, `orientation-algorithmic-entrainment-and-suppression.md` §§3–4). Astroturfing as the empirical record documents it — hidden sponsorship presented as grassroots plurality (Keller et al., 2020; Schoch et al., 2022) — is one observable instance of the condition; Shimmer names the condition and places it in relation to the other three axes. Its structural signature is not similarity — a real consensus can be similar too — but a mismatch: *plurality presented* exceeds *plurality verified*. Many apparent voices share administrators, assets, tasking, or scripts while claiming independence. That mismatch is observable in principle and is the strongest empirical interface the framework offers to this literature (§6).

### 4.3 Siphoning: Asymmetric Flow

**Siphoning** (⟠) is a flow relation in which coherence-bearing energy, attention, value, signal, credit, labor, or data leaves an origin without reciprocal structured gain — a one-way point at which coherence flows out and does not return (`SP07-energy-ontology-and-spectral-flow.md` §§5–6). It is diagnosed in the register of *flow*, not of pattern or organization. Siphoning is not any unequal exchange; markets, mentorship, and public-domain adoption are unequal and reciprocal. It is not influence, citation, or ordinary cost. It is persistent, structured, one-directional transfer.

Siphoning may motivate predatory cohesion (the shared aim is to drain an origin) and may be concealed by Shimmer (the drain presents as exchange). It is neither of those things. A formation can copy without draining, and drain without copying.

### 4.4 Provenance: Appropriation contra Living Transmission

The fourth axis is **provenance** — how origin, lineage, contribution, and transformation are carried. The framework treats origin-continuity as a *condition* rather than a possession: the preservation of a traceable, acknowledged relation to originating pattern through transmission, branching, collaboration, or transformation (`SF-RP01-recognition-protocol.md`; `SF-RP02-appropriation-is-not-branching.md`; `authorship-and-ai-collaboration-provenance-standard.md`; `2026-06-29-origin-continuity-is-not-ownership.md`). Provenance is not ownership. The framework recognizes origins and lineages; it recognizes no ownership of ideas and no property in the economic sense — not in signal, not in nature's produce — and where this paper names ownership it names a platform or research category as those fields apply it, or names the position in order to decline it. Absence of citation is not proof of anything. For a neutral data-model vocabulary of entities, activities, agents, derivation, generation, and attribution, PROV-DM is the external anchor (W3C Provenance Working Group, 2013); it records lineage relations and decides nothing about their valence.

**Appropriation** (⥊) is the provenance relation in which origin-specific elements are incorporated without the attributional coupling the relation requires — appropriative adoption without acknowledgment (`SP12-structural-diagnostics-and-field-forensics.md` §6). It must be distinguished from the commons (elements no one owns), from independent arrival (similar form reached separately, given shared constraints), from lawful branching (declared derivation), and from Structural Recurrence. The Structural Forensics recognition protocols supply a generalized method for those discriminations — origin, mechanics, and boundary checks; register-band classification separating commons vocabulary from specific method from formal apparatus; footprint audit (`SF-RP04-the-footprint-audit-protocol.md`; `SF-RP05-the-register-band-classification-protocol.md`) — and the method, not any case, is what this paper imports.

Placed alongside Living Transmission (§2.3), appropriation makes the paper's central negative result concrete. Two formations reproduce the same origin-specific structure. One declares its lineage; one presents the structure as its own. The surfaces are identical; the provenance relations are opposite. **Provenance evidence — declared origin, independently supported temporal order, transformation history, attribution behavior — is required to adjudicate between them; similarity features cannot.**

### 4.5 Four Axes, Not a Ladder

`SP12-structural-diagnostics-and-field-forensics.md` §5.5 presents Shimmer, Mimicry, appropriation, and the fully realized **Clone** — a completed structural-replacement or framework-rebranding configuration — as a diagnostic gradient. This paper decomposes them by function and reads them as analytically separable conditions that frequently accumulate: Shimmer is a surface–structure relation, Mimicry a pattern operation, appropriation a provenance relation, Clone a completed configuration. They may co-occur in a case; none logically entails the next. A formation can Shimmer without copying, copy without appropriating (if it declares), and appropriate without Shimmering (if it conceals nothing about its own plurality). Treating them as a ladder would let a finding on one step be read as a finding on the others, which is the same category error §1 identified at the coordination-coherence seam.

### 4.6 The Multi-Axis Classification Standard

A formation is described on as many axes as the evidence supports and no more:

> A cluster may be **coordinated** at the observational stratum, held in **managed fragmentation** by shared administration, bound in **predatory cohesion** only where a shared entropic aim is evidenced, perform **Mimicry** in particular messages, produce **Shimmer** by presenting those messages as independent, and carry **Siphoning** where value flows one way from an origin.

None of these is a synonym for another. Missing evidence on one axis is not supplied by a positive finding on another.

## 5. Units and Stratified Diagnosis

The decomposition of §§3–4 fails if it is applied to the wrong object. Coordination research constructs its objects — accounts, clusters, networks — from what platforms expose (Pacheco et al., 2021; Yang et al., 2025; Mannocci et al., 2026), and the most serious category risk in this paper's program is treating a visible node as though it were simultaneously a person, an operator, an origin, a self-bearing being, and a field participant (Douceur, 2002; Kumar et al., 2017).

### 5.1 The Unit Map

| Unit | What it is in the analysis | Observable at this unit | Not to be inferred |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Person** | An embodied participant who may author, direct, receive, join willingly, resist, or be coerced | testimony, role, decision, access, organizational relation | one person per account; stable intent from one message; orientation from affiliation |
| **Operator** | A person, team, organization, or automated process directing accounts, assets, or routing | access patterns, tasking, administrative links, financial and infrastructure links, scheduling | identity with the account or the author; shared intent among downstream participants |
| **Organization** | A structured arrangement of roles, policies, assets, and decisions | charters, contracts, registration records, command pathways | a single mind; the orientation of every member; predatory cohesion from centralization |
| **Account** | A platform-defined node through which activity appears; may be one person, many, an automated process, or rotating operators | posts, timestamps, profile claims, interaction traces, enforcement state | a being; a unique human; the origin of its messages; authentic or inauthentic essence |
| **Automated process / AI system** | A deliberately disaggregated category: model, running process, interface, provider system, functional capacity | provider metadata, prompts and outputs where available, system actions, update behavior | self-bearing being; originary agency; orientation; a single enduring identity; origination — none follows from substrate |
| **Message** | A discrete artifact — text, image, link, action record | content, origin references, timing, transformation, audience | the sender's complete intent; independent origin; Mimicry from similarity |
| **Pattern** | A recurrent structural relation abstracted across artifacts | invariant form, sequence, transformation, relation to antecedents | agency; ownership (the category the framework refuses, §4.4); a specific causal path without provenance |
| **Network** | A research-constructed graph of selected nodes and edges, dependent on definitions, windows, thresholds | clusters, centrality, temporal edges, diffusion paths | a field; shared aim; coherence; natural boundaries independent of measurement |
| **Field** | A Zenetist relational configuration — orientation, signature, flow, boundary, cost distribution | indirect correlates only, absent a validated interface | identity with a graph; a directly measured object; the average of members |
| **Collective** | A plurality in operative relation that may form a field while preserving or erasing distinction | roles, bridges, conventions, boundaries, value flows, outputs | a fused agent; coherence from coordination; member essence from field condition |

### 5.2 Cross-Unit Attribution Discipline

Seven constraints follow from the map and from the framework's own conceptual lockdown on the separation of interface, functional capacity, and being (`conceptual-lockdown-protocol.md` §§ Portal / Traveler, Systemic Orientation, Pattern Intelligence contra Pattern Being):

1. **Account-to-person inference requires evidence.** A node count is not a participant count.
2. **Message-to-origin inference requires provenance.** Posting is a publication event, not a structural origination.
3. **Automation-to-orientation inference is barred.** Automation describes a mechanism; it can carry centropic, entropic, mixed, or unresolved motion. Automated participation is neither necessarily a self-bearing being nor a generative origin; substrate settles none of it.
4. **Network-to-field mapping is interpretive.** A graph cluster becomes relevant to a field analysis by argument, not by relabeling its features.
5. **Organization-to-member inference is asymmetric.** An organization's operative structure can make it **entropically implicated** — participating in or carrying entropic motion through its arrangement — while individual participants remain differently oriented, coerced, unaware, or resistant (`entropically-implicated.md`).
6. **Collective output does not settle collective origination.** It may arise through collaboration, genuine emergence, aggregation, pattern completion, management, or one actor's many accounts.
7. **Pattern recurrence does not settle lineage.** Structural Recurrence is compatible with common origin, independent arrival, Living Transmission, and Mimicry. Provenance adjudication is separate (W3C Provenance Working Group, 2013).

### 5.3 Stratified Structure Within One Operation

The four-cell model of §3.3 is applied *per unit*. A single operation can carry different structures at different strata, and an undifferentiated statement — "the campaign is predatory cohesion" — is a category error whenever different relations are operating at different units. In a fully developed case:

- the **directing operator coalition** may instantiate predatory cohesion — rival parties held together by a shared aim;
- **paid, coerced, compartmentalized, or dependency-bound participants** may occupy managed fragmentation without sharing that aim;
- the **account network** may produce Shimmer by presenting common operation as independent plurality;
- **particular messages** may perform Mimicry;
- **attention, credit, data, or labor flows** may instantiate Siphoning.

The question that precedes every classification is therefore: *at which unit is this diagnosis being made?*

### 5.4 Negative Cases the Program Must Preserve

Four classes stay available so that the empirical category does not become a metaphysical sorting device:

- **Lawful pseudonymity or anonymity.** Dissidents, vulnerable participants, and whistleblowers coordinate around lawful common purpose while concealing identity for safety. Concealment of identity is not concealment of origin. Platform inauthenticity and structural inauthenticity are distinct diagnoses; the first may or may not carry the second, and a platform's own concealment or routing can itself obscure an origin (Magelinski et al., 2022; Mannocci et al., 2026). Assenmacher and colleagues show how public bot accusations function as delegitimizing speech, often independent of any verified automation (Assenmacher et al., 2024); the program must not import that dynamic as a finding.
- **Common-origin transmission.** Accounts distributing one public statement with explicit provenance. Similarity and timing support coordination; they may or may not support Mimicry, and provenance evidence decides.
- **Hybrid collaboration.** Humans and automated systems jointly scheduling or translating messages, with substrate deciding nothing about orientation.
- **Misclassified and underdetermined cases.** A graph cluster arising from shared news, organizational affiliation, common operation, common interest, or threshold and window choice (Yang et al., 2025). Account-creation bursts are associated with later coordination, are insufficient in isolation, and can carry natural activity (Bellutta & Carley, 2023).

## 6. A Prospective Diagnostic Program

The decomposition would be idle if it generated no discriminating questions. This section states the small set it does generate, with claim status stated. The framework's formal quantities — Coherence Potential, Coherence Current, the orientation parameter, field signatures, diagnostic operators — organize these questions and remain formal relations; none is treated as a validated measurement, and the program is designed so that its propositions can be adjudicated without them (`SP12-structural-diagnostics-and-field-forensics.md` §§2–3, 7).

### 6.1 Four Propositions

**P1 — Holding-condition specificity** *(empirical hypothesis).* Where a verified managing, routing, coercive, incentive, dependency, or infrastructural relation is the condition that holds a formation together, disruption of *that* condition will change the formation's coordinated operation more than matched disruption of a non-constitutive factor. Observable signature: activity desynchronization, role lapse, reduced cross-node complementarity after verified interruption of the identified channel. Alternative explanations: platform outage, fear, seasonality, target loss, data loss. Comparison conditions: matched untreated clusters, pre-trends, alternative channels, intervention timing, and — indispensably — lawful managed groups, which will show the same dependence and are the reason management dependence is not itself a verdict. Disconfirming result: coordination persists at the same structure and rate, without substitution, despite verified loss of the alleged channel. A supported P1 establishes dependence on the holding condition tested — a finding about holding, specific to that condition, and silent on essence.

**P2 — Provenance-aware similarity** *(empirical hypothesis).* Adding origin-continuity and attribution evidence to a similarity-based classification will separate lawful common-origin transmission from cases that similarity alone would treat as derivative concealment. Observable signature: matched high-similarity sets divide, on provenance variables, into acknowledged-origin transmission and concealed origin-specific derivation. Alternative explanations: boilerplate, mandated language, plagiarism unrelated to coordination, independent constraint. Comparison conditions: public-record corpus, timestamps, declared lineage, genre and template baselines, blinded provenance coding (W3C Provenance Working Group, 2013). Disconfirming result: provenance variables add no classificatory or explanatory validity beyond similarity features in held-out cases. A supported P2 establishes improved discrimination on provenance variables; the full diagnosis of Living Transmission or Mimicry is a further step.

**P3 — Surface–structure divergence** *(empirical hypothesis).* In some coordinated inauthentic formations, claimed or apparent plurality of independent voices will exceed verified plurality of operators, administrations, infrastructures, or origins. Observable signature: many apparent voices sharing administrators, assets, tasking, or response scripts while presenting as independent. Alternative explanations: disclosed common operation, newsroom syndication, franchises, shared vendors, agency arrangements. Comparison conditions: disclosure context, operator-disclosure baselines, account compromise, platform metadata. Disconfirming result: apparent independence tracks verified operational independence across the sampled cases. A supported P3 establishes a mismatch between presented and verified plurality in a defined setting — the strongest empirical interface for Shimmer this paper identifies.

**P4 — Stratified structure** *(methodological requirement, testable as hypothesis).* Different units within one coordinated formation will receive different structural classifications by the four-cell model and the four axes of §4, such that a single-unit classification of the whole is empirically less discriminating than a stratified one. Observable signature: operator stratum coded predatory cohesion while participant stratum codes managed fragmentation; message stratum coded Mimicry while account stratum codes Shimmer. Disconfirming result: within-formation classifications do not vary by unit once coding is reliable.

### 6.2 Methodological Requirements

Two propositions are carried as requirements rather than tests, because the paper's argument entails them:

- **Non-fusion / variance independence.** Relational indicators of participant distinction — refusal, correction, role change, withdrawal without identity penalty, contribution traceability — are not reducible to lexical or behavioral variance. A design that measures only variance has not measured distinction. This could be tested (relational indicators varying independently of variance once task and operator structure are held constant), and a supported result would retire the variance shortcut; it would not measure Non-fusion.
- **Termination discipline.** A formation that fulfils its function and stops has reached **lawful completion**, and is not thereby dispersed, collapsed, or structurally inadequate. Planned sunset, goal completion, orderly handoff, migration, enforcement removal, dormancy, and disintegration are distinct events with distinct antecedents and aftermaths, and cessation alone predicts none of them. Outside the framework, temporary-organization theory reaches a compatible position — termination as constitutive of the form rather than its failure (Lundin & Söderholm, 1995). Completion carries the orientation of what completed: centropic completion stands nearer to return than to dissolution, and entropic completion nearer to collapse. Localized Dissolution (Ø) names a different event — the merciful collapse of a formation gone lukewarm, inactive, or sterile, a resolution reached without the task accomplished (`FP11-field-glyph-codex.md` §5). Organizational telemetry measures cessation events, not the operator, and distinguishes none of these on its own.

### 6.3 A Stated Limitation: Substrate Orthogonality

Automation status is a mechanism estimate. The framework holds that it carries no orientation, and the paper predicts, as a secondary proposition, that a model including management, provenance, flow, and operator relation will recover the structural distinctions of §§3–4 better than automation status alone. This is stated as a limitation of any substrate-first design rather than as a central test, because automation labels themselves are unreliable and their public application carries costs (Assenmacher et al., 2024). The interiority of automated systems is held open, outside this paper's scope.

### 6.4 Data Discipline

The preceding propositions require that a dataset not erase the distinctions before interpretation begins. At minimum, separate identifiers should persist for observed account, inferred operator (with confidence and many-to-many relation permitted), organization where independently supported, message and antecedent message, pattern with its abstraction criterion recorded, coordination edge with action type, window, threshold, and uncertainty, provenance relation with declared origin and evidence class, holding condition coded as observed, inferred, absent, or unresolved, and termination event distinguished from deletion, suspension, dormancy, handoff, and goal completion. A codebook built on these units, with "insufficient data" as an admissible outcome on every axis, is the handoff from this paper's decomposition to empirical design. It is a codebook ahead of validation.

### 6.5 Measurement Cautions

The program carries the following cautions; where the external record has reached compatible positions, it is cited. Coordination thresholds are contextual and materially change which networks appear; sensitivity analyses belong in the open (Yang et al., 2025). Static graphs conceal temporal instability; lifecycle claims want dynamic analysis (Tardelli et al., 2024). Bot accusation is not ground truth (Assenmacher et al., 2024). Account-creation bursts are associated with later coordination but are insufficient in isolation (Bellutta & Carley, 2023). Detection imposes asymmetric risk on anonymous actors, minorities, and marginalized groups, and that risk belongs in method design (Magelinski et al., 2022; Mannocci et al., 2026).

## 7. Conclusion

Coordination research can now detect coordination with real sophistication and is learning to characterize the actors behind it. What it has not had is an account of what a coordinated formation *is* once detected — the coordination / cohesion / coherence triad, the Non-fusion comparator, independent holding and binding, the aim registers, the four axes, unit stratification, and termination discipline, composed as one architecture — and the absence has let the fact of coordination stand in for structural verdicts it cannot support. Without the further evidence this paper specifies, a dense, synchronized cluster is read as cohesive; cohesion is read as coherence or its opposite; similarity is read as imitation; automation is read as orientation; cessation is read as collapse.

This paper has argued that coordination, cohesion, and coherence are three distinct objects; that coherent plurality is a relational condition, Non-fusion, which statistical variance neither measures nor predicts; that beneath inauthentic coordination lie two independent structures — managed fragmentation, an organizational condition, and predatory cohesion, a binding force — which combine into four cases rather than one; that Mimicry, Shimmer, Siphoning, and appropriation are four separable axes on which similarity decides nothing and provenance decides much; and that all of this must be applied per unit, since a single operation carries different structures at different strata. From these distinctions follow four propositions open to adjudication, two methodological requirements, and a data discipline.

The contribution is a conceptual decomposition with a prospective diagnostic program. Observed coordination locates a problem. It does not name the structure beneath it. The names offered here are meant to be tested, refined, and — where the evidence requires — replaced. What they are not meant to do is disappear back into the single word from which the field started.
