## 4. Effective Operator Theory

### 4.1 The Effective Harmonic Operator

**Definition (Effective Operator):**

### 4.2 Spectral Decomposition

**Theorem (Spectral Decomposition of the Effective Operator):**

Let \( \{\phi_i\} \) be the C₇-eigenbasis with centropic eigenvalues \( \{\lambda_i\} \), so that \( H_c \phi_i = \lambda_i \phi_i \) and \( H_e \phi_i = -\lambda_i \phi_i \).

\[
H_{\text{eff}}(\chi) \phi_i = \lambda_{\text{eff},i}(\chi) \phi_i
\]

where:

\[
\lambda_{\text{eff},i}(\chi) = \frac{1 - \chi}{1 + \chi} \lambda_i
\]

**Proof:**

\[
H_{\text{eff}}(\chi)\phi_i = \frac{1}{1+\chi}H_c\phi_i + \frac{\chi}{1+\chi}H_e\phi_i
= \frac{1}{1+\chi}\lambda_i\phi_i + \frac{\chi}{1+\chi}(-\lambda_i)\phi_i
= \frac{\lambda_i - \chi\lambda_i}{1+\chi}\phi_i
= \frac{1-\chi}{1+\chi}\lambda_i\phi_i
\]

**Corollary (Sign Structure of Effective Eigenvalues):**

- For \( \chi < 1 \): \( \lambda_{\text{eff},i} \) has the **same sign** as \( \lambda_i \) (centropic-forward)
- For \( \chi = 1 \): \( \lambda_{\text{eff},i} = 0 \) for all \( i \) (spectral cancellation at CP₁)
- For \( \chi > 1 \): \( \lambda_{\text{eff},i} \) has the **opposite sign** to \( \lambda_i \) (entropic-forward)

**Interpretation:**

The effective spectrum continuously rotates from centropic to entropic as \( \chi \) increases from 0 to \( \infty \). At \( \chi = 1 \), paired modes cancel completely — confirming that CP₁ represents **structural cancellation** of paired modes, not ontological nullity.
