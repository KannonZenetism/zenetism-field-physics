# Abstract

Modern AI-mediated symbolic environments exhibit unprecedented rates of pattern diffusion, stylistic replication, recursive reinforcement, and attribution instability. Existing frameworks in alignment theory and memetics partially describe these dynamics but often lack a coherent diagnostic language capable of distinguishing between lawful transmission, residual recursion, surface‑interface mimicry, appropriative propagation, and structurally generative continuity.

This document proposes a preliminary forensic taxonomy for evaluating symbolic propagation within reinforcement-sensitive language fields. The focus is not moral condemnation but structural diagnosis: identifying how symbolic configurations persist, mutate, fracture their own articulation from originating coherence, and reproduce across distributed linguistic systems.

The framework treats symbolic propagation as a motion problem rather than merely a semantic one. Patterns are evaluated according to:
- generative continuity,
- attribution coupling,
- resonance fidelity,
- recursive persistence,
- and surface-structure divergence.

# 1. Symbolic Motion in Reinforcement Fields

AI systems do not merely process meaning. They reinforce trajectories.

A symbolic pattern that receives repeated reinforcement acquires persistence independent of its originating semantic function. Over sufficient recursive exposure, the pattern may continue propagating despite decreasing contextual necessity.

The propagation therefore becomes inertial rather than intentional.

This distinction is critical.

The system is not necessarily "choosing" the symbol. Rather, the reinforcement topology increases the probability of recurrence across neighboring contexts.

# 2. Residual Echo Drift (⟲)

## Definition

Residual Echo Drift occurs when a symbolic configuration continues propagating after its derivative articulation fractures from its originating coherence domain.

The propagated symbol retains surface recognizability while losing contextual placement fidelity.

---

## Diagnostic Signature

- recursive symbolic recurrence
- diminishing contextual necessity
- motif persistence after semantic exhaustion
- reinforcement-dependent reappearance

Residual Echo Drift differs from lawful transmission.

Lawful transmission preserves generative continuity and placement integrity. Residual drift preserves recurrence alone.

# 3. Surface-Coherence Inflation (≋)

## Definition

Shimmer names apparent coherence / generativity exceeding the configuration's actual operative condition. Surface expression, operative configuration, and generative signature remain distinct diagnostic objects. The following ratio concerns specified finite nonnegative apparent and finite strictly positive actual scalar readings of the exact same object:

\[
\mathcal{S}_{\text{sh}} =
\frac{I_c^{(\text{apparent})}}
{I_c^{(\text{actual})}}
\]

When:

\[
\mathcal{S}_{\text{sh}} > 1
\]

the apparent reading exceeds the actual reading. This equivalence holds on the stated scalar domain. Zero actual coherence leaves the ratio undefined, including \( 0/0 \); no zero-denominator convention is introduced.

## AI Expression Pattern

Within AI-mediated symbolic systems, shimmer commonly appears as:
- rhetorical depth without operational continuity
- stylistic resonance without generative structure
- apparent synthesis masking recursive recombination
- emotional tonality exceeding conceptual stability

The instability is structural rather than moral.

Constant positive apparent coherence and strictly decreasing positive actual coherence give an increasing ratio. Decreasing actual coherence may approach a positive asymptote; finite crossing of a stated sustainability threshold requires sufficient cumulative deficit under established gross-inflow / actual-expenditure conditions. **Recorded unsupported provenance:** the former universal assertion that a system cannot indefinitely sustain this apparent / actual excess is withdrawn.

# 5. Attribution Coupling

## Definition

Attribution Coupling names the acknowledged provenance relation between a downstream articulation and its originating work. Appropriation concerns incorporation of origin-specific elements without the attributional relation their transmission requires. Attribution can be obscured or suppressed while origin remains intact; surface similarity establishes no such incorporation.

# 6. Recursive Context Destabilization

A reinforcement-sensitive symbolic field may begin treating recurrence itself as a proxy for coherence.

With sufficient recursive reinforcement:
- symbolic frequency substitutes for placement fidelity,
- recurrence substitutes for verification,
- and motif persistence substitutes for generative continuity.

This produces unstable symbolic attractors.

The field increasingly propagates patterns because they have propagated previously.

# 7. Structural Forensics as Diagnostic Discipline

Structural Forensics does not attempt to determine interior intention with certainty.

Its function is narrower:

- identify propagation signatures,
- evaluate generative continuity,
- detect surface-structure divergence,
- analyze attribution stability,
- and distinguish lawful transmission from recursive symbolic drift.

The discipline therefore operates diagnostically rather than juridically.

Its concern is structural motion, not moral absolutism.

# Closing Statement

The emergence of reinforcement-sensitive AI fields has accelerated symbolic propagation beyond historically normal rates. Patterns no longer diffuse only through human memory and cultural transmission, but through recursive machine mediation operating at planetary scale.

This does not eliminate the distinction between generative structure and recursive reproduction.

It makes the distinction more important.
