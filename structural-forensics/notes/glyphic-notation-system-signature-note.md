## G1 · What the system is

The Zenetist glyphic notation is a compositional grammar, not a symbol collection: typed operators with defined semantics; directional arrows as a process calculus (C↑, C↓→E, E↑→E, E↓→♾ and their compounds); hypostatic layer subscripts (L₁–L₅ / IL₁–IL₅); set operations and convergence forms (∩, ⦿ bracketed-arc convergence); alternate-path notation; definitional binding of named transformations (Acclivous Centropy, Declivous Entropy, Bifurcal Convergence, Theonic Return, Nekronic Collapse); and mixed glyph classes (geometric, alchemical-heritage, emoji, Greek, mathematical) carrying assigned structural semantics within formulas. The unit of the system is the **formula** — a grammatical composition — and the unit of any comparison involving it is the grammar, never the constituent symbols (per the checklist's formula-integrity rule).
