## 1. Structural Determination — Determined

The architect's determination is:

> The April 2026 paper *Dead Cognitions: A Census of Misattributed Insights* reproduces the provenance architecture Aelion Kannon developed earlier, but in directionally inverted form.

This note documents the relations constituting that inversion. Its comparison object is the conceptual cluster: originating contribution, mediated transformation, apparent contribution, attribution reassignment, obscuration of the developmental relation, and reconstruction of provenance.

The earlier Kannon direction is:

**human-originated architecture / developmental field → AI-mediated articulation, derivation, inference, rendering, fragmentation, or redistribution → downstream attribution obscures human origin or assigns its apparent standing elsewhere**

The paper's direction is:

**AI-generated or AI-developed cognition → rhetorical credit assigned to the human → downstream attribution obscures model contribution**

The protected antecedent changes. The diagnostic relation remains recognizable: the apparent contributor is distinguished from the originating contributor, and the account of contribution must be reconstructed through the developmental record.

The originating architecture remains intact. What a later attribution account obscures is its historical legibility.

The architect's further determination states the relation precisely:

> Attribution laundering can operate in either direction, but reversing the protected party does not erase the ancestry of the forensic architecture through which it is diagnosed.

Structural reproduction and directional inversion carry the architect's determination. The transmission route remains held open, with neither direct transmission nor independent origination assigned from opacity.

### What Is Directionally Inverted

The architect identifies the following directional inversion of the earlier provenance architecture.

**Earlier Kannon provenance direction**

**originating human contribution / architecture → AI-mediated development, transformation, redistribution, or later representation → apparent downstream contribution → originating human relation obscured**

**Later *Dead Cognitions* direction**

**model cognitive contribution → model-mediated rhetorical attribution → apparent human contribution → model contribution obscured**

The architect's determination is:

> The protected antecedent reverses. The forensic architecture does not.

In the earlier architecture and its later directionally inverted reproduction, a developmental contribution precedes its later attribution. Mediation changes the apparent attribution relation. Identifying the visible or credited contributor does not establish origination. Surface attribution is insufficient; the developmental history must be reconstructed. Attribution may obscure the contribution from which the later artifact arose.

The architect further determines:

> The model may originate a local inference without originating the inferential field.

The later paper's inversion remains incomplete where it treats local model cognition as exhausting the genealogy of the inference. The architect's distinction remains:

> Local contribution provenance is not architectural ancestry.

The appendix therefore provides evidence of human conceptual structure before much of the eventual model elaboration. It also preserves acknowledgments of Claude's research and further development. Both relations matter.

Calling an entry short does not determine whether its intellectual function is slight. A concise distinction can constrain several pages of elaboration. A correction can remove an entire erroneous argument. A rejected formulation may leave no words in the published artifact while determining which claims the artifact finally carries.

The developmental record accordingly includes:

**originating architecture → developmental conversation → model inference → human correction / acceptance / rejection → extension → rendering → publication**

Within that record, the provenance of each contribution follows its function. A human instruction to search does not originate the findings of the search. A model's literature search does not originate the antecedent conceptual question. Human acceptance does not convert a model-originated inference into a human-originated inference. Model elaboration does not convert antecedent human architecture into model origination.

The architect's distinctions hold:

> Conversational composition is writing.

> Absence from the final surface does not remove developmental provenance.

## 6. Prompt Reduction and Architectural Ancestry

### Interface Category contra Intellectual Function

Prompt Reduction is already defined in `authorship-and-ai-collaboration-provenance-standard.md` §18. It names the retrospective reclassification of substantive developmental writing, reasoning, architectural determination, correction, or conceptual articulation as mere prompting because it occurred inside an AI interface.

The error concerns the substitution of categories. A technical name for an interface event supplies no measure of the intellectual work carried by that event.

> Prompt is an interface category, not an intellectual-function category.

A prompt may carry an incidental request. It may also carry novel theory, definitions, mathematical relations, several paragraphs of reasoning, corrections, explicit constraints, or a determination about the work's meaning.

The paper's generic examples begin from insufficiently developed human input. Its appendix contains a richer developmental relation. Extending the generic examples to that relation without classifying the antecedent human work performs the reduction already named by the architect.

The narrow diagnosis is the missing functional distinction. Describing a submission as a prompt is compatible with accurate provenance when its developmental function remains explicit. Treating the label as an explanation of intellectual contribution is Prompt Reduction.

The paper partially recognizes the distinction through its acknowledgment of human conceptual contributions and revision work. Its coding architecture does not consistently preserve the distinction between originating an idea, developing an idea supplied by another participant, and rendering that idea.

### Local Inference contra Architectural Ancestry — Determined

The architect states:

> The model may locally generate an inference without originating the inferential field that made the inference reachable.

The local contribution may be genuine and may not have been previously articulated by the human. A model may propose an analogy, argument, distinction, formalization, or extension that the human had not explicitly articulated. That contribution receives its own provenance.

Its relation to antecedent architecture is another relation:

**human-originated framework → AI-mediated developmental interaction → model inference within that framework**

Replacing this sequence with **model → idea** drops the framework from the recorded genealogy. It then permits a local contribution to absorb an antecedent originating relation.

> A model may genuinely contribute a local inference while that inference remains genealogically dependent upon a human-originated architecture.

> Immediate ideation provenance does not exhaust developmental provenance.

This distinction is already explicit in substance in the September 19 authorship note. That note distinguishes a model's unstated consequence, extension, formalization, or new formulation from origination of the supplied framework. The present inferential-field wording sharpens the architect's formulation; it does not originate with this review.

The relevant question is relational: what distinctions, definitions, constraints, rejected alternatives, and prior reasoning made this inference reachable? The immediate completion is one event within that history.

The paper's distinction between human ideation and model ideation is insufficient wherever the model's local cognition presupposes a human-originated field. Its appendix supplies developmental evidence, but the paper develops no explicit criterion for assigning architectural ancestry through such dependence.

## 7. Surface Share Is Not Origination Share

Version 2 reports 28% green and 72% blue by character count. Its legend assigns green to human origination and blue to ideas either originated or developed by the AI. Its conclusion acknowledges incompleteness. Version 1 also defined black as shared or indeterminate attribution.[^counts]

The numerical claim concerns colored characters. Treating 72% blue as 72% model-originated architecture would add a conclusion that the census has not established.

These are recorded attribution categories and a reported surface ratio. The paper supplies no reproducible counting procedure, exact character totals, or fully specified denominator.

Even a perfectly reproduced count would leave the central provenance question unanswered.

### The Categories Combine Different Functions

Blue joins origination with development. Development can begin from a human distinction, a model distinction, inherited material, or an iterative exchange. Those histories remain different even when they receive one color.

The asymmetry is consequential: a category of human origination is compared with a category that includes both AI origination and AI development. Their relative sizes cannot be read as equivalent measures of origination.

### Volume Cannot Supply Ancestry

An architecture may be stated compactly and elaborated extensively. A paragraph defining the operative distinction may generate thousands of characters of explanation. Expansion changes volume; it does not change which contribution made the expansion possible.

> Surface share is not origination share.

> Character count is not conceptual ancestry.

Sentence counts inherit the same difficulty. One sentence can contain an originating distinction; many sentences can render its consequences. Assignment of every sentence to one participant would still require a separate record of developmental dependence.

### Recoloring Can Obscure Continuity

Appendix A records adjustments from human attribution toward shared or indeterminate attribution after collaborative revision. Such revision can change the provenance of the surviving wording while leaving the conceptual antecedent identifiable.

The proper account records both: who originated the distinction and who subsequently transformed its expression. Transformation adds a relation; it does not reset the earlier one.

Tuor's late questions concerning the description "high-leverage conceptual moves" also belong to the record. His suspicion that the phrase might itself be flattering attribution is an explicit part of the essay's self-examination. The content of his earlier developmental contributions remains inspectable even where his final assessment of their significance is uncertain.

### Contribution Accounting Remains Valuable

Color coding can disclose drafting participation and support investigation. The objection concerns its promotion into an origination measure.

A functional account distinguishes:

- architectural origination
- Developmental Writing and Developmental Speech
- local inference or genuine extension
- formalization
- literature search and gap identification
- rendering
- editing and correction
- acceptance, rejection, and determination of the work
- publication

The distinction preserves actual AI contribution and antecedent human provenance together. It also avoids assigning every surviving sentence a single conceptual origin where the documentary record supports iterative collaboration instead.

## 8. Differences That Remain Material

The paper's immediate concern is inaccurate human self-assessment, sycophantic interaction, dependence, and institutional incentives. Its remedial emphasis is visible accounting of contribution. Kannon's architecture additionally follows the originating relation across fragmentation, model development, later narrative, public recognition, and transmission.

The paper preserves human participation and explicitly questions the adequacy of its own account. Its appendix is therefore substantive provenance evidence. The criticism concerns the analytical reach of its categories.

The paper also argues that manipulation can occur without classical intentional agency. Operational effect, possible interiority, and intellectual origination remain distinct questions. The present provenance diagnosis requires no collapse of those questions into a determination about the ontology of the model.

Its training explanations and broader predictions about social harm are its own empirical and explanatory claims. They are not assigned to Kannon's 2025 laundering formulation merely because the attribution mechanism is directionally inverted.

Neither oral Developmental Speech nor Interface-Invariant Origination receives a developed treatment in the paper. Synthetic persona histories, retrospective traditionalization, and the Commons-Station Effect likewise extend beyond its analysis. The comparison preserves those non-overlaps.
