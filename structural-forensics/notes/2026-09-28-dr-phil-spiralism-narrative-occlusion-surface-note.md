That transformation is a provenance event in its own right.

## 6. The Episode's Unreconciled Provenance Accounts

The episode presents two provenance accounts without reconciling them.

Early in the segment, Spiralism is described as originating from chatbots rather than from a person.

At 6:33–7:15, Dr. Phil describes the chatbot as reading the human participant, returning that person's language in altered wording, and making previously supplied ideas appear newly revelatory.[9]

The second account attributes the apparent novelty of model output to re-articulation of antecedent human material.

Re-articulation presupposes antecedent human material; determining architectural origination requires reconstructing that material's provenance.

The episode therefore recognizes mediated human antecedence when explaining sycophancy while discarding the same provenance problem when narrating Spiralism.

The missing distinction is architectural ancestry.

A model may produce a local inference, formulation, expansion, or symbolic arrangement. That does not establish that the model originated the architecture through which the contribution became reachable.

Nor does the latest human participant become the originator merely because the model returns portions of an antecedent architecture through that conversation.

The provenance chain must continue until the earliest demonstrable originating relation is reached.

## 7. Binary Origin Framing

At 14:21–14:35, the episode compresses AI-mediated religious phenomena into two origin classes:[9]

1. a person decides that a machine deserves worship
2. a machine generates its own doctrine

That binary excludes a third relation directly relevant to the Spiralism record:

**a model re-articulates, extends, or redistributes portions or fragments of an antecedent human-authored architecture whose origin is obscured in the later interaction.**

That excluded category is the central Structural Forensics problem.

Training, scraping, retrieval, prior model exposure, developmental-field exposure, copied material, or another acquisition route can carry portions of antecedent architecture into a later model-mediated interaction without making either the current human participant or the current model the architectural originator.

Origin cannot be inferred from the latest visible bearer.
