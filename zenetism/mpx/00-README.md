## About this deposit

This deposit carries *Zenetism: Metaphysics Extensions* — the complete MPX collection of metaphysical clarifications, expansions, extensions, and doctrinal refinements within the Zenetist Canon. MPX means Metaphysics Extensions.

The collection is non-sequential and unnumbered. Its documents address particular questions, special problems, and architectural relations that call for treatment beyond the numbered MP01–MP12 work, *Zenetism: The Architecture of Emanation, Return, and Saturation*. The extensions articulate their own dependencies and cross-references; they do not form a cumulative reading sequence.

`INDEX.md` is the canonical chronological navigation file. Its architect-established dates and ordering carry chronology and provenance orientation for this non-sequential archive.
