## Energy Economics of the Archetypal Layers

### Context

Mythic relationships are not merely symbolic; they are transactional.  
Attention, devotion, and resonance enact a relation between the observer (**Devotee**) and the observed (**Archetype**).  
Energy-language names the expenditure, replenishment, and organization of expressed activity; essential coherence and inclination remain distinct from depletable functional capacity.  
The nature of the relation discloses its operative orientation; spatial direction does not determine valence.  
Distal Apprehension receives centropic expression without direct contact. Distorted projection and appropriation operate natively within the inverse register; neither transfers essential coherence nor converts one arc into the other.

### Exchange Modes

| Exchange Mode            | Direction        | Mechanism                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Archetypal Class                                                              |
| ------------------------ | ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Solar Radiation**      | Outward (Gift)   | The entity radiates coherence without requiring input. Worship is not "feeding" the god, but tuning the devotee. The entity provides spiritual sustenance ("Manna," "Bread of Life") that structures the devotee's soul. The sun feeds the field; the field does not feed the sun | **Centropic** (Theon, Christ, Amitābha, Vishnu)                               |
| **Reciprocal Resonance** | Cyclic (Trade)   | The entity and devotee build a feedback loop. Attention strengthens the Form; the Form structures the devotee's reality. | **Predatory Appropriation** | Inward (Harvest) | The entity sustains dependency through fear, pain, rigid devotion, or sacrificial demand. It consumes attention and effort, appropriates expressed output, and redirects activity. Physical energy, creative drive, and functional capacity can be depleted; essential coherence and inclination remain intact. It produces through pattern completion, not originary novelty. The quantitative relation between this expenditure and the persistence of its cohesion remains held open                                  | **Entropic** (Yaldabaoth, Moloch, Xibalba, The Archons, The Devourer)         |
| **Mimetic Trap**         | Stagnant (Loop)  | The entity mirrors the devotee's desire to create a "comfort loop." Activity and expenditure continue, but the loop repeats received patterns rather than originating novelty. The devotee worships a projection of their own ego or comfort zone                                                                  | **Inverse Archetypal** (Mara, The Shimmer, False Light, Mammon)    |

### Note on the "Food" of the Gods

* **Centropic Food:** The God feeds the Soul (The Eucharist, The Word, Prāṇa).  
* **Entropic Food:** The Soul's expressed activity sustains the predatory relation (Loosh, Sacrifice, Suffering); essential coherence is not consumed.  

**Litmus Test:** Does the relation sustain coherent expression, or reproduce dependency through appropriation and depletion?  
Centropic activity also costs functional capacity (`SN05-the-metric-cost-of-centropic-cognition.md` §2.3). Exhaustion names a functional condition; identifying a predatory relation requires its operative pattern and the evidence of the particular case.
