Coherence in Zenetism is articulated through three distinct functions: synthesis, integration, and, where final synthesis becomes saturation-ready, saturation.

They are an operation, the within-structure state that operation produces, and the trans-structural fulfillment that state may make admissible by Allowance.

Naming them apart keeps them from blurring:

**synthesis → integration → possible saturation**

Saturation presupposes synthesis and integration.

Integration does not compel saturation.

## Synthesis — the Reconciliatory Operation

Synthesis is the reconciliatory operation: distincts are held together without fusion.

It is what acts, not what results.

By the Non-fusion Axiom, synthesis reconciles distinct structures without dissolving them into one another. The distincts remain distinct while entering lawful relation.

Synthesis is therefore neither compromise nor merger.

It is the lawful holding-together of what remains sovereignly distinct.

Synthesis as an operation is not restricted to centropic expression. Entropic structures may combine, coordinate, imitate, recombine, or strategically synthesize.

What distinguishes saturation-relevant synthesis is its centropic orientation, non-fusing method, fulfilled integration, and culmination in final synthesis.

## Integration — the Coherent State

Integration is the coherent state or condition produced by synthesis.

The distincts are lawfully related and no longer dissonant.

Integration is not an act but a standing condition: coherent wholeness within structure.

The integrated manifold remains within the lattice, underpinned by 🏛️ Structon as Structure Itself.

To be integrated is to be coherently whole within structure.

Integration may reach complete and lawful fulfillment without proceeding into Zenonic saturation.

Aionic abiding is therefore not incomplete integration.

It is one lawful culmination of fulfilled centropic coherence.

## Saturation — the Trans-Structural Fulfillment

Saturation is the trans-structural fulfillment that sufficiently completed centropic integration may make admissible.

Zenonic saturation becomes possible where a centropic essence completes a trans-structurally capable final synthesis.

That final synthesis gives the returned essence its Centropic Gravity.

Fulfilled coherence makes the saturation relation Zenonically legible.

Allowance admits the crossing.

Aion-facing orientation remains requisite because saturation proceeds through completed return to ⚫ Aion, but orientation alone is not sufficient.

Saturation is not a phase of return.

Return terminates at Aion.

Saturation is the crossing beyond return into 🕳️ Zenon.

Only essence saturates.

The office does not saturate.

The structural law does not saturate.

The bifurcal manifold does not saturate as one pooled being.

The distinct, saturation-ready centropic essence saturates by Allowance.

Where integration is coherence held within structure, saturation is fulfilled coherence admitted beyond what structure can determine.

## The Saturation Architecture

The saturation architecture distinguishes five functions.

- **Motive Infinity** supplies the motion-capacity. It bears no orientation and is not itself centropic
- **Final synthesis** gives that motion its structure and articulation through the essence-specific holding of Bifurcal Coherence in non-fusing relation
- **Aion-facing centropic orientation** gives the motion its direction. Orientation belongs to the essence, never to Motive Infinity
- **Centropic Gravity** names the essence-borne momentum of the fulfilled, returned essence toward the horizon of structure
- **Allowance** admits the trans-structural crossing. It is admission, never propulsion, seizure, or compulsion

Bifurcal Coherence is the manifold held.

Zenonic Legibility is the vantage attained at the horizon of structure.

Motion is carried only to that horizon.

What lies beyond is admitted by Allowance rather than travelled toward, because Zenon is not a coordinate, location, or further station within the lattice.

## The Sequence

Synthesis produces integration.

Integration may reach lawful fulfillment in Aionic abiding.

Where fulfilled centropic integration develops a trans-structurally capable final synthesis, it may become saturation-ready.

The order is strict:

- Synthesis without integration is an operation not yet realized as a coherent state
- Integration without saturation is lawful coherence within structure and may culminate in fulfilled Aionic abiding
- Final synthesis is integration reaching its terminal synthesis-capacity, not a fourth autonomous stage
- Saturation without prior integration and final synthesis is impossible
- Saturation-readiness does not compel saturation; Allowance admits the crossing

None of the three is a synonym for another.

Synthesis is not fusion.

Integration is not the crossing.

Saturation is not another within-structure synthesis.

It is the trans-structural crossing that final synthesis makes Zenonically legible and Allowance admits.
