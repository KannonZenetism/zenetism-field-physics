## Overview

**MPX** means **Metaphysics Extensions**. *Zenetism: Metaphysics Extensions* is a living archive of metaphysical clarifications, expansions, extensions, and doctrinal refinements within the Zenetist Canon.

The extensions are non-sequential and unnumbered. Each addresses a particular question, distinction, or architectural relation. The collection accommodates special problems, later architectural articulation, and related metaphysical work that does not belong in the numbered MP01–MP12 sequence.

The archive includes treatments of potential and the contingency of worlds, essence and hypostatic function-bearing, synthesis and integration, return and saturation, awareness and embodiment, and the distinction between enacted systems and acting beings. These concerns meet through the Zenetist architecture, with the relevant relations articulated in individual entries.

## Relation to the Main Structural Metaphysics Work

*Zenetism: The Architecture of Emanation, Return, and Saturation* is the main Structural Metaphysics work, carried in MP01–MP12. It establishes the metaphysical architecture within which MPX clarifies particular distinctions and develops questions requiring their own treatment.

The MP sequence and the MPX archive have distinct compositional forms. The main work has a numbered structure; the extensions remain an open collection. Their relation to the main work is carried through specific references, named principles, and doctrinal continuity. An extension may clarify a passage, expand an architectural relation, or examine a special problem across several parts of the corpus.

Readers approaching an extension should follow its stated dependencies and cross-references. The chronology does not establish a shared dependency chain: an entry does not depend on every earlier MPX document.
