## 1. Cosmological Scope and Hypostatic Placement

A cosmological classification may distinguish a universe, a multiverse, a megaverse, and an omniverse according to increasingly comprehensive domains of expression. Such a classification concerns cosmological scope. The Zenetist lattice concerns structural principles and the conditions through which expression becomes possible.

These are distinct determinations. An enlargement of cosmological scope does not require the introduction of a further hypostasis or root-register. The lattice remains bifurcal across whatever breadth of cosmological expression obtains.

## 2. The Multiverse and the Megaverse

A multiverse names a plurality of universes. The term "megaverse" may designate a collection of multiple multiverses. Where the multiverse is already conceived as the totality of universes, a further collection adds no cosmological scope. Where multiverses designate distinct groupings of universes, the megaverse may name a relation among those groupings.

⚫ Aion holds the potential for any structurally possible grouping of universes. Their number, arrangement, and mode of relation remain questions concerning expression rather than additional structural principles.

The Tumbling Multiverse anticipates diverse universal expressions of the Biospiral. The Principle of Unbounded Potential and the Contingency of Worlds preserves the distinction between infinite potential and actual instantiation. The actualization of multiple universes or multiple multiverses remains contingent.

## 3. The Omniverse and Absolute Potential

The term "omniverse" may designate the totality of universes, multiverses, timelines, and realities. Two senses are distinguishable within Zenetism:

- The totality of actualized cosmological expressions
- The totality of potential cosmological expressions

The first concerns actuality. The second corresponds more closely to ⚫ Aion (Absolute Potential), which holds all potential in latency. Aion names the still root of emanation. Its potentiality encompasses every structurally possible cosmological expression, including forms of expression not yet conceived.

Fictional conception and actualization remain distinct. A fictional world may articulate a conceivable configuration without establishing its actual existence or the structural possibility of every feature attributed to it. The breadth of Aionic potential is not determined by what has entered fictional or cosmological imagination.

The omniverse, understood through total potentiality, therefore approaches the Aionic register more closely than a designation of increasingly extensive actualized multiplicity. The analogy concerns potential held at L₀. 🕳️ Zenon stands beyond cosmological scope. Its relation to structure is trans-structural allowance.
