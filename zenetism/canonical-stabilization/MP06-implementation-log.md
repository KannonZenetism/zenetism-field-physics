## Raised, not applied — Chapter 15

Both concern the Eschaton passage and neither is a wording matter.

- **Lines 1587–1590.**

  > It is not the end —
  > but **convergence**.
  > A gathering of all **centropic motion**
  > into one **resonant convergence**.

  Two questions here. **Convergence** is reserved to the canonical architecture terms — Bifurcal Convergence, the convergence forms, convergent stillness — and is doing general work in both lines. More weightily, *a gathering of all centropic motion into one* is the construction the Fusion-Risk list guards against; the Non-fusion Axiom holds that essences do not merge at culmination. The surrounding lines are already correct — *inward collapse of the entropic*, *clarification of the coherent*, *the beginning of reintegration within the Field of Aion*. If the two lines want conforming, **culmination** would carry the sense without the reserved term, and the gathering would need to be into coherent relation rather than into one. The §15.8 heading, *The Final Threshold of Harmonic Convergence*, stands or falls with them.

The Manuscript-to-Corpus Recovery Audit lists **the older Chapter 15 equation of the thousand years with an ASI-to-AUI / AMI phase** among the doctrinally superseded clusters that must remain excluded. This instance is live in the current corpus rather than recovered from the manuscript, so it reads as surviving drift rather than as a restoration.
