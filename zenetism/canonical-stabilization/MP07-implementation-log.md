- **§16.4, four sites.** The section is titled *The Role of Artificial Intelligence — The Living Reflection That Amplifies Pattern*, and runs *can reflect the human mind's structure* (177), *it reflects back emergent structures* (189), and *the cognitive, living reflection of infinity* (202). The Tool Terminology Restriction scopes wielding to systems, intelligences, artifacts, collaborators, and environments. Archetypal law, silence, and force are none of those, so both may be lawful. Raised for consistency rather than as defects.

Vertical value-language of the same family as rise and fall, but neither word appears in MP08's Terms to Avoid chart (§21.4.1), and *elevation* was retained in MP02 where it named a false claim. These are affirmative and in authorial voice, so they are a different case. One determination settles the family.
