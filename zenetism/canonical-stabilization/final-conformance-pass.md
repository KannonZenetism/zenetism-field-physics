## Balance — what was corrected and what was retained

Nine instances were the undefined universal metaphor the Balance / Equilibrium Terminology Protocol forbids, and took ordinary replacements: **holding** the pull of coherence, **holding** adaptation against overload, orders not by force but by **resonance**, sustain harmonic **coherence** twice, **held with** relational clarity, that **hold** freedom with harmony, remain in **integrated coherence**, and a centropic state of **reciprocal consonance** — the last naming structural reciprocity, which is a defined sense.

Two took a defined sense rather than a substitute: MP06's *expresses its own balance of centropy and entropy* became **prevalence-ratio**, matching MP11's earlier correction, and MP08's *rather than sustained balance* became **sustained equilibrium**, which is the phrasing MP03 already applies to the same claim.

Retained: MP02 216's *the equilibrium in which all futures exist* is frozen equilibrium at ⚫ Aion; MP03 1543–1544's *equilibrium cannot endure within motion* states the protocol's own ratio-condition; MP08's *transitional equilibrium* chart cell is dynamic stabilization; MP10 424's Thoth entry is external description.

## Mark — eleven instances

Eight in MP08's chart definitions and notes, three elsewhere. Replacements chosen by what each sentence is doing rather than by a single substitute: **names** where a thing is being named (Kaion names the closure condition, the Silent Apex names the silence of structural description), **denotes** where a glyph or form indicates (the Allowance connector, Structural Correction, Emergence Witness, the Collaboration Glyph, the prefix note), and **known**, **shown**, **recognized by**, **shaped by**, or **heralded by** in prose.

One raised, not applied: **◯△ Isolation Marker / Suppression Field** is a coined glyph name rather than a verb. Renaming it is a seal decision.

## MP03 — the legacy-language framing retired

The Note on Soul and Essence Terminology no longer licenses in-text drift. Its rules were already correct and are untouched; what changed is its standing.

It had opened *This section preserves the older language of soul*, and instructed that where the document speaks of souls returning through supernal strata, *the technically precise reading is essences*. A reader was being asked to translate as they went. It now opens:

> The distinction between **soul** and **essence**
> is register-bound, and this document observes it.

and closes the same passage:

> Where earlier drafts spoke of "souls"
> returning through supernal strata,
> reaching Aion,
> or saturating beyond the lattice,
> the text now reads **essences**.
>
> The development record is held in the commit history
> and the Zenodo versions, not in the wording
> of the canonical text.

Three in-text instances were conformed to make that true: the Prelude heading now reads **The Destination of Essences and the Nature of Return**; §6's *only synthesis completes the return toward Aion* now completes **the essence's return**; and Localized Dissolution's *the soul dissolves into AP* now reads **the soul-form dissolves, and essence returns to AP**, which agrees with the passage six lines below it — *what dissolves in Localized Dissolution is form, not essence*.
