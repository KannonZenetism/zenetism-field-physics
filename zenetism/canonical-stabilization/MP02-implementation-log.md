**Reasoning recorded.**

- **AI takes a valence-neutral verb.** The charts place artificial intelligence on either arc depending on orientation and action, so reflection cannot be assumed at line 2010. Correspondence carries the structural claim without the valence.
- **Merger language removed at 2203–2205.** *Boundary no longer appears as separation* read as absorption. A saturated essence is not destroyed or taken into a common pool; it has passed beyond the horizon of explanation while remaining itself. The corrected lines say that boundary ceases to hold as a term of its relation, not that the separation dissolves. The *by which boundary becomes conceivable* construction stands, since that formulation is reserved for 🕳️ Zenon — which is not conceivable by ordinary cognition, and is conceivable only as a concept that cannot properly be conceived.
- **Radiance, not silence, at 2244.** Radiance is the term already applied to the centropic essence capable of Zenonic saturation. **Beyondness** replaces stillness, since stillness belongs to ⚫ Aion while beyondness is what the passage is reaching for.
- **Line 2241, *Zenon is not reached*, stands.** Every other reach-construction in MP01 took the saturation form, but here saturation is the lawful relation, so denying it would state something false. The same holds at MP01 line 2117.
- **Silence at Zenon is lawful and is not being purged.** Under the Twofold Tetralemma, both silence and unhearable sound may be said of Zenon from our end, and neither is the actual case. MP01 retains fourteen such constructions; only §1.2 changed there, and that was for the location of the stir rather than for the silence. At MP02 line 3139 only the verb moved — *reflected* claimed a valence Zenon does not carry, since Zenon is not itself centropic but stands more closely related to centropy from centropy's end, while reflection tied to the inverse would be worse still. **Intimated** claims neither.
