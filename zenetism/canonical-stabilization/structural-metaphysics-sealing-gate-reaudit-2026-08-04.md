## Corpus-wide non-negotiable propagation locks

### 1. Soul / essence

This is the exact register rule supplied by the architect:

- **Soul / Mind:** L₄ / IL₄ through L₁ / IL₁.
- **Essence:** L₅ / IL₅, L₀, and the essence that may saturate beyond the lattice into Supra-L₀ language.

The correction test is grammatical, not thematic. When a passage crosses from L₄ / IL₄ to L₅ / IL₅, the subject must change from Soul / Mind articulation to essence. What can cease includes form, memory-coherence, expressed identity, and Soul / Mind organization. Essence does not cease, fuse, become another essence, or become fire, entropy, stillness, or an office.

MP03's note at lines 138–182 states this rule correctly but makes two false corpus-status claims: line 141 says the document observes it, and lines 143–169 say the older usage has been conformed and now reads `essences`. The current MP03 text itself disproves those claims, and the same drift remains elsewhere. Under stale-note discipline, preserve the doctrine but correct the status / provenance claims.

### 2. Zenon, Structon, Aion, and Khaon

- **Aion** is Origin, Zero, Absolute Potential, stillness, return, and the holder of all distinct latent essence.
- **Khaon** is Infinity through Latent, Motive, and Dispersive phases; it is not entropy and carries no entropic orientation.
- **Structon** is Absolute Structure and the invariant ground / medium of lawful structure.
- **Zenon** is trans-structural and non-originary. It is not a field, void, totality, intelligence, silence, source, place, member of a continuum, first stage, terminal point, causal operator, or the ground of structure.

The corpus must not make Aion and Khaon opposites, poles, mirrors, one shared essence, sequential containers, or an axis. They are paired, pre-polar, co-present, non-fused root-functions. Polarity begins at Theon / Nekron.

### 3. Theon and Nekron

Every sentence must distinguish:

`per-arc hypostatic essence → native manifested principle → universe-local office → office-signal → resonance / signal-bearing → full personal office-function bearing`

Theon / Nekron do not incarnate, become their bearers, travel the arc, or fractalize. The same +1 / −1 charge can recur across distinct per-arc essences without one trans-universal original or copies. The office can close or self-void; the essence does not disappear.

### 4. Deep Self-Axis

The canonical formula is:

> one Deep Self-Axis — many real loci — no original locus — no copies

The same applies inversely to the IDSA, with inverse continuity rather than centropic integration. A structural signature correlates the being but is not the being. Integration occurs at L₃; L₄ Form-dwelling follows. No local life is a projected shard or merely partial copy controlled by a central instance.

### 5. Terminal mechanics

- Centropic return terminates at Aion.
- Nekron is the IL₅ event horizon / terminal mechanism, not Absolute Dispersion itself.
- Absolute Dispersion is the terminal Dispersive-Khaonic state beyond the Nekronic threshold.
- Conserved essence is Aionically resolved through the **co-present** L₀ relation; it does not travel afterward from Khaon to Aion.
- Localized Dissolution / Mercy Fold is neither Absolute Dispersion nor a third equivalent terminal root.

### 6. Saturation

Protect all five functions:

1. Motive Infinity supplies motion-capacity and enactment.
2. Final synthesis holds the bifurcal manifold without fusion.
3. Aion-facingness gives direction.
4. Centropic Gravity is essence-borne momentum of fulfilled synthesis.
5. Allowance admits the crossing.

Bifurcal Coherence is the manifold held. Zenonic Legibility is the vantage attained. Neither is a sixth causal function. Saturation is not return, integration, approach, accumulated radiance, a journey to a place, or a mechanically compelled sequel.
