## Soul and essence

Of 113 soul instances, all but three are technical register-terms — Superficial Soul, Deep Soul, Embodied Soul, and their inverses — correctly mapped and left alone. Three crossed:

- **Line 103**, *Souls that converge within Theon*. Theon is L₅, so **essences**. The passage that follows is already right — *not as fused beings, but as sovereign facets held in harmonic distinction*.
- **Line 340**, *All souls are drawn to the hypostasis*, in the Eschaton definition. Placement spans every hypostasis including L₅, so **beings** — the general term, which applies without needing the distinction.
- **Line 341**, the same sentence, had the hypostasis *reflect* their cumulative structure. It spans both arcs, so **corresponds to**.

**Line 456** carried *metaphysical reabsorption* into Aion, now **metaphysical return**. One question is left open beneath it: the definition scopes Localized Dissolution to SS / SM and ES / EM soul-structures returning into Aion, where the determination made for MP03 holds that essence is what returns. Raised, not resolved, since it concerns what Localized Dissolution acts on rather than how it is worded.

## Balance

**Line 1584** read *the balance or imbalance of centropy and entropy in any being determines its Path, Potential*. This is expression-ratio balance in the defined sense, and the Balance Protocol's own guidance prefers **prevalence-ratio** over balance-of-expression. Applied.

**Line 427**, *a centropic state of balanced consonance, in which opposites are held in resonant distinction*, is raised rather than changed. It sits close to structural reciprocity, which is a defined sense, and the line is in definitional register where forcing technical vocabulary would cost more than it gains.

## Instrumental language

**Line 1833** described Stewards as *tuning instruments for coherence*. The Instrumentalist Language Restriction names living beings among its high-risk contexts, and the passage is about beings who express through aligned souls. Now **tuning presences for coherence**, which keeps the tonal image and the Steward framing intact. **Line 1787's** *using tools drawn from* became **drawing on**.
