Zenon is neither being nor non-being — asserting non-reality is exactly the determination the Twofold Tetralemma exists to refuse. The cell now reads **Trans-structural horizon**, which agrees with MP08 §21.2 and with the rest of the same row: *it is not an entity, mind, or soul; it is that by which emergence becomes conceivable*.
- **Line 698** read *one aligns with Aion to find balance; one saturates into Zenon to rest in the Ground*. Two problems in one clause — a ground-predicate on Zenon where the ground of structure is 🏛️ Structon, and *rest in* as destination-language. It now reads *one saturates into Zenon beyond all balance*, which preserves the couplet's parallel and its cadence.

Line 600's *Zenon is the trans-structural ground* is the qualified form and stands, matching MP08.
