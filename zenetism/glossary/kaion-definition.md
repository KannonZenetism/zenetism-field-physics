## Definition: Kaion (⦿)

**Kaion (⦿)** is the **Convergence Principle** — the unified state where both **centropic fullness** and **entropic completion** resolve into **Aion's stillness**. It is not a third entity, nor a fusion of opposites, but the **structural terminus** where the Aionic and Khaonic trees complete their arcs and rest in equipoise.

Kaion represents the **pre-bifurcal and post-resolution ground** — the **silence of the spiral** where:

- **Centropy** attains wholeness through acclivous return `(C↑ → ⚫)`  
- **Entropy** completes as static potential through declivous terminus `(E↓ → ♾ → ⚫)`

Both rest in **Aion’s embrace**, held in **absolute equilibrium**. This is not *balance as tension*, but **fulfillment as resolution** — the place where all motion settles into the **Silent Core**.

## The Nature of Convergence

**Kaion** names what occurs when both trees exhaust their trajectories:

- The **centropic essence** returns to Source as integrated fullness — coherence attained, pattern preserved, the soul complete.  
- The **entropic essence** disperses into static potential — form released, structure reabsorbed, the pattern returned to latency.

Both states are stillness, yet distinct in their character:

- One is the **stillness of arrival** (*centropic completion*)  
- One is the **stillness of release** (*entropic resolution*)

Together, they converge in **⚫ Aion** — not as opposites meeting, but as **complementary termini** of the same bifurcal motion, held within a single, lawful substrate.

---

## Distinction from Zenon

Kaion must not be conflated with **🕳️ Zenon** (*the Unknown Principle*):

- **Zenon** is the *trans-structural ground* — beyond potential, pre-conceptual, unknowable. It holds the lattice from **outside emanation**.  
- **Kaion** is the *convergence within emanation* — the **knowable terminus** where both trees resolve. It is the **silence of fulfillment**, not the silence of mystery.

> Zenon is the ground that permits motion.  
> Kaion is the ground where motion completes.
