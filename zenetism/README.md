## Registers

The work moves between two registers, and they follow different rules.

**Poetic register** carries most of the twenty-six chapters. Short lines, hard breaks, heavy bold. The line structure is doctrine, not decoration — it is never reformatted into technical prose.

**Definitional register** carries the Symbol Key charts and the glossaries. Definition cells operate as structural descriptors rather than as sentences, so terminal punctuation is deliberately omitted even where internal punctuation is present.

Notes sit inside sections as `#### Note on <Subject>`, followed by prose, then a glyphic sequence line, then the section divider.

---

## Conventions a reader or collaborator needs

**Glyphs** appear in the Symbol column of charts, in glyphstream lines, in process strings, in seals, and as glyph plus name inside definition cells. They do not appear in running prose or in note bodies.

**Soul and essence** are register-bound and the distinction is load-bearing. Soul, in the strict sense, begins at the Soul / Mind bifurcation at L₄ / IL₄ and runs through L₁ / IL₁. Essence pertains to L₅ / IL₅, to L₀, and to what saturates beyond. A soul reorients, refines, and remembers; an essence is what returns.

**Supernal** and **subversal** name positions, not arcs. They cover the bands L₅ through L₂ and IL₅ through IL₂, and they sit on the arc without comprising it. The arcs themselves are centropic and inverse, or Aionic and Khaonic.

**Reflection** is centropic. Mirroring, echoing, and mimicry are entropic. Where a sentence needs only the ordinary sense of *reflect*, the word is *expresses* or *indicates*, so the valence is not spent on a neutral construction.

**Zenon** is trans-structural. It is not an emanative source, not the ground of structure — that is Structon — not a destination, and not a location. What can be said of it is said from our side of the limit and never as a determination of Zenon itself.

The vocabulary and formatting of this corpus are held in a small set of files. They apply across all six disciplines and live outside this folder, in `../the-zenetist-canon/canonical-stabilization/`:

- `compact-architecture-revision-audit-guide.md` — the condensed audit reference, with the sentence test and the validation checklist

Corrections follow these files, not precedent in the text. Any engagement with this material is subject to the attribution condition stated below.

Note that two folders share the name **canonical-stabilization**. The one under `the-zenetist-canon/` holds the four standards, which apply corpus-wide across all six disciplines even though the metaphysics files themselves sit here rather than there.
