## Abstract

Neural metabolism conditions the continuation of embodied cognitive activity. Its quantities include substrate inventories, reaction rates, vascular delivery, and expenditure across specified intervals. Structural Cost names a different relation: native coherence expenditure. A physical ATP budget can articulate a material condition of embodied capacity without becoming a measurement of essential architecture or orientation.

This extension distinguishes ATP amount from concentration, turnover, and chemical power; examines preparation-specific neuronal and astrocytic metabolic evidence; and specifies how expenditure and performance require independent observations. A typed ATP balance demonstrates that equal inventories can accompany unequal turnover. A conditional depletion calculation identifies its own time and capacity limits. Rest enters as reduced expenditure, with no second entry for the same saving. These constructions retain physical consequence while preserving the distinction among material support, operative access, observable expression, and essential Mind.

## 2. Amount, Concentration, Turnover, and Power

### 2.1 Distinct Physical Quantities

| Quantity | Definition and example units | Required distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ATP amount | Molecules or mol inside a specified boundary | Inventory is distinct from the speed of replenishment |
| ATP concentration | Amount per volume, such as mmol L⁻¹ | Equal concentration can accompany unequal compartment volumes |
| ATP synthesis or hydrolysis rate | Amount formed or consumed per time, such as mol s⁻¹ | A rate must be integrated across time to obtain an amount |
| Tissue-normalized ATP rate | Amount per tissue mass per time, such as μmol g⁻¹ min⁻¹ | The denominator and tissue composition must remain explicit |
| Glucose or oxygen metabolism | Specified substrate consumption per tissue amount per time | Substrates, pathways, and reaction yields remain distinct |
| Cerebral blood flow | Blood volume per tissue mass per time, such as mL (100 g)⁻¹ min⁻¹ | Delivery volume does not state oxygen content or tissue consumption |
| Chemical power | Chemical free-energy release per time, J s⁻¹ | Power is distinct from energy integrated across an interval |

For ATP amount \(A\), compartment volume \(V>0\), and concentration \(c\), the relation \(A=Vc\) requires compatible units. If volume changes, concentration can change even when ATP amount is constant. Conversely, a stable concentration in a growing compartment need not indicate a stable amount. The measurement record must therefore identify which quantity was obtained.

An ATP concentration also omits its replenishment and consumption rates. Rangaraju and colleagues measured presynaptic ATP with an optical reporter in cultured hippocampal neurons. Activity-dependent synthesis accompanied synaptic demands; brief interruptions impaired vesicle functions. The evidence concerns local terminals and calibrated observations, and illustrates why a maintained ATP pool can require continuing synthesis. [M2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24529383/).

### 2.2 A Typed ATP Balance

Let \(A(t)\geq0\) denote ATP amount in one declared compartment, measured in mol. Time \(t\) is measured in seconds. Define the following rates, all in mol s⁻¹:

| Symbol | Physical meaning |
| --- | --- |
| \(J_{\mathrm{syn}}\) | Net ATP formation through the separately represented synthesis pathways |
| \(J_{\mathrm{hyd}}\) | Net forward reaction extent for the represented ATP-to-ADP hydrolysis pathway |
| \(J_{\mathrm{other}}\) | Signed ATP change through any additional explicitly represented reactions |
| \(J_{\partial}\) | Net ATP transfer into the compartment across its boundary |

On the interval considered, synthesis and net forward hydrolysis are nonnegative. Additional reactions and boundary transfer can have either sign. Each reaction enters exactly once. The accounting relation is

\[
\frac{dA}{dt}
=J_{\mathrm{syn}}-J_{\mathrm{hyd}}
+J_{\mathrm{other}}+J_{\partial}.
\]

This is a physical specialization of the LM08 typed balance, with the boundary and reaction terms declared. The symbol \(J_{\mathrm{other}}\) does not conceal an unexplained mismatch: the included reactions must be named, or their omission justified for the interval. When their aggregate contribution is unknown, the budget is incomplete.

A compartment inside a cell can exchange ATP with another part of that cell. A model spanning different cells requires evidence for each selected transport relation. It cannot silently insert cross-cell ATP transfer to represent glial support. A lactate-transfer model instead carries lactate amounts and the relevant production, transport, and oxidation reactions. Glucose, lactate, ATP, and joules cannot be added as though they were one quantity.

### 2.3 Equal Inventory, Unequal Turnover

Consider two stipulated accounting cases with the same positive initial ATP amount. In each case, set \(J_{\mathrm{other}}=J_{\partial}=0\), and hold the represented rates constant:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{Case A:}\quad
J_{\mathrm{syn}}=J_{\mathrm{hyd}}
&=1\ \mathrm{pmol\,s^{-1}},\\
\text{Case B:}\quad
J_{\mathrm{syn}}=J_{\mathrm{hyd}}
&=2\ \mathrm{pmol\,s^{-1}}.
\end{aligned}
\]

The balance gives \(dA/dt=0\) in both cases. After ten seconds, the represented hydrolysis amounts are 10 pmol and 20 pmol, respectively, while the inventories remain equal. These values are illustrative assumptions, not measured brain data. Continuing synthesis is already part of each case.

The calculation establishes a precise limitation: an ATP amount does not determine turnover. It also leaves performance unresolved. No task variable appears in the equation. A conclusion about accuracy, memory, or restored access would require an independently specified observation and a tested relation to the physical budget.

### 2.4 Chemical Power Has Its Own Conditions

For the specified hydrolysis reaction, let \(\Delta_r G_{\mathrm{hyd}}\) denote reaction Gibbs energy change in J mol⁻¹ at the stated temperature, pressure, and chemical composition. With \(J_{\mathrm{hyd}}\) defined as its net reaction extent rate,

\[
P_{\mathrm{hyd}}
=-\Delta_r G_{\mathrm{hyd}}J_{\mathrm{hyd}}.
\]

Negative reaction Gibbs energy change and positive net forward hydrolysis give positive released chemical power in J s⁻¹. If forward and reverse reactions are both appreciable, their net extent is required for this expression. A gross forward event count does not include the opposing contribution. Distinct reactions require their respective energy changes and rates.

ATP amount is therefore not an energy quantity in joules. The chemical reaction and its conditions complete that conversion. The displayed power concerns the represented chemical process; it is not a measurement of all tissue heat, cognitive work, or native Structural Cost. Changing chemical conditions can change power even when the molar rate is unchanged.

## 3. Cellular and Vascular Participation

### 3.1 Maintenance and Signalling

Howarth and colleagues calculated neural ATP budgets from anatomical and electrophysiological information. The allocations differed between cerebral and cerebellar cortex and changed when action-potential assumptions were revised. Their analysis supports process-specific accounting while retaining uncertainty about firing, cellular properties, and maintenance expenditure. [M1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3390818/).

For a proposed embodied articulation, this requires an explicit partition of the physical processes being compared. Maintenance of an available circuit and its participation in a selected task can both require expenditure. A task-associated increment is a comparison with an identified baseline, not the complete metabolic history of the operation. Changing the baseline changes that increment.

The relevant physical boundary may include neurons, glia, extracellular space, and vascular exchange. Selecting only one participant changes the balance being described. An apparent gain inside the selected boundary may be a transfer from another participant; an internal transfer cancels only when the encompassing account includes both participants in compatible material units. LM08 §6.3 supplies that accounting distinction.

### 3.2 Glucose and Lactate: Conditional Pathways

Lundgaard and colleagues examined a glucose analogue in awake mice. Neuronal uptake increased with sensory stimulation, and anesthesia altered the observed pattern. This supports direct neuronal participation while retaining the distinction between analogue uptake and a complete metabolic accounting. [M3](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms7807).

Díaz-García and colleagues combined neuronal biosensors with pathway interventions in slices and awake mice. Their stimulation-related results supported neuronal glycolysis without requiring neuronal lactate uptake. The measured redox transients do not constitute a complete ATP budget or exclude lactate participation in every condition. [M4](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28768175/).

Theparambil and colleagues identified an astrocytic adenosine A₂B-receptor pathway supporting metabolic activation and lactate availability in animal preparations. Receptor disruption affected synaptic and behavioral functions. This supplies evidence for a particular astrocytic support relation, without establishing that all neuronal fuel passes through that route. [M5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07611-w).

These observations address different preparations and interventions. The appropriate comparison retains cell identity, substrate availability, stimulation, acquisition interval, and the measured response. An exclusive universal shuttle would erase these conditions. A declared pathway model can instead ask when a participant contributes, what material crosses the boundary, and which operation depends on that contribution.

### 3.3 Delivery Is a Distinct Relation

Attwell and colleagues reviewed neuronal and glial participation in cerebral blood-flow regulation, including the importance of oxygen conditions. Their account establishes a broader delivery context; it does not supply one fixed conversion from vascular response to cognitive function. Detailed pathway claims require their own preparation-specific evidence. [M6](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature09613).

For the present accounting, blood flow, substrate concentration, transport, and reaction rate remain separate variables. A delivery change and a consumption change can occur together, but their causal relation must be examined. An encompassing average can also omit a localized constraint. The selected scale determines whether a particular bottleneck is visible in the record.

## 4. Expenditure and Performance

Jamadar and colleagues distinguish ongoing metabolic demands from task-associated changes and examine differences between glucose and hemodynamic imaging. Their review supports recording task, baseline, and modality separately. A regional tracer observation does not provide every subsequent metabolic reaction or an exhaustive cognitive assessment. [M7](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2024.11.010).

The proposed physical comparison therefore has two independently defined sides: expenditure and outcome. Expenditure might be integrated ATP hydrolysis in a compartment during an interval. Outcome might be discrimination accuracy across repeated trials. Their relation requires paired observations. Increasing the former does not define improvement in the latter.

An efficiency ratio also needs a declared output criterion. Correct responses per measured expenditure answers one question; retained information across a delay answers another. Altering the task, baseline, observation duration, or excluded bodily costs changes the comparison. Such ratios describe their selected physical and behavioral relation. They do not classify essential architecture or native orientation.

Equal task scores likewise leave several possibilities unresolved. One physical configuration may maintain the score with different expenditure, response latency, recovery demand, or susceptibility to interruption. The claim of compensation requires evidence about the changed means of achieving the outcome. SN12 §§7–9 supplies the architectural distinctions; this extension adds the material accounting needed to investigate their cost.

## 5. Depletion, Restoration, and Rest

### 5.1 A Conditional Depletion Result

Consider a finite interval with constant rates, no boundary transfer, and no additional ATP-changing reactions. Let

\[
D=J_{\mathrm{hyd}}-J_{\mathrm{syn}}>0.
\]

Here \(D\) is a stipulated net deficit in mol s⁻¹. Integration gives

\[
A(t)=A_0-Dt.
\]

For an independently specified operational threshold \(A_{\mathrm{crit}}\), with \(0\leq A_{\mathrm{crit}}<A_0\), first attainment occurs at

\[
t_{\mathrm{crit}}
=\frac{A_0-A_{\mathrm{crit}}}{D}.
\]

The units are seconds. This calculation applies only while the constant-rate assumptions hold, and it must not be continued to negative amounts. The threshold is a model input; a biological application must establish what operation it predicts and with what uncertainty.

Actual changes in synthesis, demand, transport, or reaction allocation can invalidate the constant-deficit description. Measuring those changes tests the proposed articulation. The result is therefore a conditional exhaustion time for a declared physical inventory, not a prediction of essential deterioration.

### 5.2 What Restores?

Restored substrate delivery, ATP inventory, synaptic operation, task performance, and anatomical integrity are distinct targets. An inventory can recover while another limiting relation persists. A task can improve through a changed physical arrangement. Neither observation reconstructs the earlier material history.

SN06 §1.3 and SN12 §§8–9 distinguish operative access from essential identity. Physical depletion can constrain expression; tissue injury can remove a particular embodied capacity. Physical restoration is assessed through the extent of repair, its timetable, and the operations recovered. The physical restoration claim must name what returned and what remained changed.

The ATP balance does not model anatomical reconstruction. The capacity construction in SN12 §9 addresses a different declared variable. Treating the two as one model would require an explicit coupling relation and empirical parameters, not a change of labels.

### 5.3 Avoided Expenditure Appears Once

LM08 §10.5 treats rest as reduced expenditure. For the accounting interval, write the represented hydrolysis rate during rest as its comparison rate minus a nonnegative saving \(S\), with \(S\) small enough to retain a nonnegative rate. Substituting that reduced demand into the balance already includes the saving. Adding \(S\) again to synthesis would count the same change twice.

This convention does not assert that all other terms remain fixed during biological rest. They require observation. It states how a specified reduction is represented. Continuing maintenance can remain costly while task-associated demand changes; replenishment requires its actual physical mechanism and material supply.

## 7. Retained Relations and Empirical Questions

The ATP articulation preserves material accounting within its boundary. Its matched-rate example proves that one inventory cannot recover its turnover. Its depletion example gives a conditional threshold time. Neither calculation contains native orientation, essential Configuration, or the Tether as a fitted variable.

The declared scope identifies what the construction can establish and which physical observations remain necessary. The research sequence can then examine paired metabolic and functional observations, changing one declared condition while tracking alternative pathways and response constraints.

| Empirical question | Discriminating observation |
| --- | --- |
| Inventory as an indicator of turnover | Joint estimates of amount and reaction rates across matched conditions |
| Dependence of a selected operation on one support pathway | Pathway perturbation with verified material consequences and an independent functional outcome |
| Local limitation within an encompassing expenditure estimate | Compartment-resolved measurements alongside the encompassing budget |
| Retained capacity with reduced task expenditure | Continued accuracy, responsiveness, and recovery across the stated interval |
| Restoration of the affected operation after replenishment | Separate records of supply, inventory, task response, and persistent impairment |
| Predictive capacity of a fitted balance | Rate and threshold predictions assessed in distinct observation intervals |

The energy relation thus remains specific: embodied cognition depends on material conditions whose maintenance and depletion can be examined quantitatively. Structural Coherence retains its native orientational and relational meaning. Connecting the two requires declared physical correspondences, with the preserved and omitted relations visible at each step.
