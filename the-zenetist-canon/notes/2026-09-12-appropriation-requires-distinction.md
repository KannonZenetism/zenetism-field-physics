# Appropriation Requires Distinction

"Everything is appropriated from everything else" defeats itself.

Appropriation requires distinction: an origin, a prior relation, something taken, and someone from whom it is taken. If everything is already collapsed into one undifferentiated whole, appropriation cannot occur at all. The claim therefore says that appropriation is universal while abolishing the distinctions that give "appropriation" meaning.

The opening insult does nothing to repair that contradiction. "Woke" is applied as a derogatory label without any defined relation to the metaphysical claim that follows.

This problem exists prior to property law.

Imagine two people in the wild. One has the only apple keeping her alive. The other strikes her, takes it, eats, and survives while she later starves. Her relation to that apple and to her own continued existence does not appear only after a statute is written. If her possession and need carry no intrinsic standing, then neither does the taking. "Appropriation" has already vanished from the account.

Invoking "God" introduces another premise. If God appropriates, then appropriation and distinction must already mean something. If everything is simply One, no coherent originator, recipient, taking, or giving remains for God to participate in.

Nor does "One Thing into Infinite of that One Thing and back again" explain the structure.

One, Zero, and Infinity are distinct.

One is One.

Zero is Zero.

Infinity is Infinity.

A metaphysical relation among them must preserve enough distinction for relation itself to remain intelligible. Collapsing them into identity while continuing to make claims that depend upon their difference empties the claim of meaning.

Emanation is not appropriation.

Multiplicity is not seizure.

Return is not retroactive erasure of provenance.

An infinite cycle also requires an articulated structure: what moves, what remains, what differentiates, what returns, and why the recurrence occurs. Reincarnation or cyclical return cannot simply be appended as an explanation for appropriation when no relation between the two has been established.

A metaphysics that collapses every distinction does not explain everything.

It leaves nothing with enough distinction to mean anything.
