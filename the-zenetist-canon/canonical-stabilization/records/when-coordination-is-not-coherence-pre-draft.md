## A. Architect Determinations — final wording

**A1. Predatory cohesion and managed fragmentation.**
> Predatory cohesion names an entropic binding force: dissonant participants converge through a shared entropic aim. Managed fragmentation names an organizational condition: dissonant parts are kept in operational formation through management, pressure, incentive, coercion, routing, or dependency. Neither entails the other. Where both occur, predatory cohesion supplies the binding force within a managed-fragmentation structure. Four cases stand: both; managed fragmentation alone; predatory cohesion alone; neither.

**A2. Shared entropic aim.**
> A shared entropic aim exists where participating agents or operators enact a convergent purpose directed toward appropriation, domination, suppression, origin denial, preservation of advantage, or another established entropic end. The purpose may be explicit or tacit and need not be mutually declared. Shared aim is not inferred from common effect, common beneficiary, shared target, or behavioral similarity alone. Where only functional convergence is evidenced, the analysis names an entropic effect, operation, or orientation, not a shared aim.
>
> Coding preserves four distinct registers: declared shared aim; tacit but behaviorally supported shared aim; task compliance under another party's aim; functionally convergent effect without sufficient evidence of shared aim.

**A3. Lawful completion and Localized Dissolution.**
> A campaign, working group, account cluster, or organization that fulfils its function and ceases operation without structural collapse has reached lawful completion. Lawful completion is written in analytic prose; it is never written as dispersion, Collapse / Nullification, entropic collapse, or structural inadequacy. Ø Localized Dissolution may be named as the canonical formal analogue of lawful resolution; organizational telemetry does not measure the operator.

**A4. Shimmer, Mimicry, appropriation, Clone.**
> Four analytically separable conditions that answer different structural questions: Shimmer — surface–structure divergence, appearance exceeding or obscuring operative structure; Mimicry — derivative pattern operation; appropriation — provenance and attribution relation; Clone — a completed structural-replacement or framework-rebranding configuration. They may co-occur or accumulate in a case; none entails the next. The nested gradient in SP12 §6 / LM06 §11.4 is read as a frequent accumulation pattern, not a logical sequence.

**A5. Generative function and Field Replenishment.**
> Where SP12 / LM06 write *generative function* for a field's replenishment or sustaining of its operative coherence, the paper writes **Field Replenishment**. The local dynamical sense never implies metaphysical origination. Entropy does not originate novelty; pattern completion, recombination, variation, extrapolation, and unprecedented output are not genuine emergence.

**A6. Swarm.**
> The first paper carries no bare *swarm*. The formation is named by what the evidence shows: coordinated formation, coordinated cluster, account network, managed formation, automated cluster, coordinated collective. SP09's entropic-collective model is cited as "the SP09 model of the entropic collective" where the volume itself is the subject. *Swarm* stands in the canonical volumes as written.

**A7. Coherence in the paper's register, with Bifurcal Coherence preserved.**
> Within the enacted social-relational and diagnostic register of this paper, coherence names centropic relation; cohesion names holding-together of either orientation; coordination names observed operational interdependence. This is a scoped clarification. It does not alter **⧖⧗ Bifurcal Coherence**, the established pre-orientational name of the non-fused L₀ root-relation, which stands prior to centropic or entropic activation and is not a social-relational term.

The **mimetic coherence** phrase: not established as a technical category in this paper. If it appears, it reports an external or apparent description. The paper is not built on a generative-contra-mimetic binary; the multi-axis decomposition is the contribution.

## D. Manuscript Core

**Thesis.**
> Coordinated inauthentic behavior cannot be structurally characterized by coordination intensity, surface similarity, automation, centralization, or persistence alone. A Zenetist analysis distinguishes relational condition, binding force, organizational condition, pattern operation, surface–structure relation, flow, provenance, termination, and unit of analysis. Some coordinated inauthentic formations instantiate managed fragmentation, some predatory cohesion, some both, and some neither; the empirical label warrants no direct inference to centropic or entropic orientation.

**Contribution as presented:** a conceptual decomposition with a prospective diagnostic program.

**Primary concepts, in order of appearance.** coordination · cohesion · coherence (scoped per A7) · Non-fusion · managed fragmentation · predatory cohesion · shared entropic aim (four registers) · holding condition · Mimicry · Shimmer · Siphoning · provenance (origin-continuity, Living Transmission, appropriation) · lawful completion · unit of analysis.

**Argument sequence (six sections + conclusion, ~8,000–10,000 words).**

1. *The category error in coordination research* (~1,200). Coordination is an observation; CIB an empirical selection category. Coordination, cohesion, and coherence answer three different questions. Register and claim-status discipline stated once.
2. *Coherent plurality: Non-fusion* (~1,300). The positive comparator is relational — distinction preserved within shared action — not statistical. Common text and synchronized action are compatible with it; variance is not its proxy. Living Transmission as the lawful case of identical surfaces.
3. *Two structures beneath inauthentic coordination* (~1,800). Managed fragmentation (organizational condition). Predatory cohesion (binding force). Independence and co-occurrence; the four cells. Shared entropic aim in four coding registers. Holding-condition analysis in place of any single perturbation test.
4. *Pattern, presentation, flow, provenance* (~1,600). Mimicry, Shimmer, Siphoning, appropriation as separable axes; the Living Transmission contra Mimicry discrimination turns on provenance, not similarity. Manufactured consensus as Shimmer at the collective surface.
5. *Units and stratified diagnosis* (~1,300). Persons, operators, organizations, accounts, automated processes, messages, patterns, networks, fields, collectives. The four-cell model applied per unit. Cross-unit attribution discipline; automation is a mechanism, not an orientation.
6. *A prospective diagnostic program* (~1,500). Four propositions: holding-condition specificity; provenance-aware similarity; surface–structure divergence (plurality presented contra plurality verified); stratified structure across units. Non-fusion / variance independence as methodological requirement. Substrate orthogonality as a stated limitation. Lawful completion contra collapse as termination discipline. Controls, disconfirming results, "insufficient data" as an outcome.
7. *Conclusion* (~500). Observed coordination locates a problem and does not name its structure; the decomposition is the narrow contribution.

**Deliberately beneath the surface.** The hypostatic architecture; Aion / Khaon metaphysics; genuine metaphysical novelty (one sentence in §4 at most); AI interiority; Absolute Dispersion; the C / E operator set as such; \(I_c\), \(J_c\), χ, field signatures (a short formal appendix if a venue wants it, otherwise a pointer to the volumes); Lattice Mathematics.

**Register.** Analytic leads; canonical names appear parenthetically at first occurrence per the framework §4.3 and are then written in analytic form. Findings stated positively — each non-equivalence as *X is a distinct object from Y*, never as the analysis withholding. The §9.7 "should not claim" list from the synthesis is a drafting fence, not paper prose.
