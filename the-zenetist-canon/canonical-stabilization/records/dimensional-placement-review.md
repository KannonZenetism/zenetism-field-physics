# Dimensional Placement Review

## Conclusion

The recorded assignments do not yet support a settled map of the full lattice. Several are plausible associations with particular hypostatic functions. The reviewed accounts do not establish a consistent criterion for calling those associations **primary**, and two existing maps distribute many operators differently.

This does not show that every recorded address is wrong. It shows why reproducing an address accurately is insufficient to validate its placement. The revised expanded diagram therefore presents the fifteen functional pairs and leaves their primary loci under review. No replacement addresses have been adopted.

That standing is already explicit in [`zenetist-analytic-vocabulary-and-accessibility-framework.md`](https://github.com/KannonZenetism/zenetism-field-physics/blob/b5869dbceb834ba180517d750795e71a2c3bfee1/the-zenetist-canon/corpus-infrastructure/zenetist-analytic-vocabulary-and-accessibility-framework.md#L895), §6.9.1: each placement needs evidence, repeated notation does not settle it, and the principal scope of the fifteen pairs remains architect-held.

## Space preceding time

The dependency in [`SP06-structural-space-orientation-paradox.md`](https://github.com/KannonZenetism/zenetism-field-physics/blob/b5869dbceb834ba180517d750795e71a2c3bfee1/structural-physics/SP06-structural-space-orientation-paradox.md), §§1–1.2, is:

**Structure → Motion → Spatial Differentiation → Orientation → Time**

The orientation term here concerns manifest orientation. The dependency does not assign one hypostasis to each term, establish chronological succession, or order the numerical dimension indices.

The recorded C₁ association at L₃–L₂ concerns continuity of identity, personal timelines, and sequential thought. The recorded C₂ association at L₂–L₁ concerns felt location, practical layout, and personal space. These are plausible applications within the layer definitions. They are narrower than the general definitions of temporal continuity and spatial relation.

Section 2 of `SP06-structural-space-orientation-paradox.md` extends structural space across both hypostatic segments, with metric extension at L₁ / IL₁. [`SP05-time-memory-hypostatic-flow.md`](https://github.com/KannonZenetism/zenetism-field-physics/blob/b5869dbceb834ba180517d750795e71a2c3bfee1/structural-physics/SP05-time-memory-hypostatic-flow.md), §3, likewise describes different temporal modes across every hypostatic register.

Consequently, the two recorded bands can be compatible with spatial differentiation preceding experienced time **if they describe selected applications**. They are inadequate as a map of where space and time first exist or everywhere they operate. Merely exchanging C₁'s and C₂'s bands would not resolve this scope problem.

The further wording tension also needs care: §1.2 of `SP05-time-memory-hypostatic-flow.md` says time is not a dimension, while Field Physics names a Temporal dimension. A possible reconciliation distinguishes **C₁ as an operation of temporal continuity** from **structural time as the relation of oriented change**. That distinction needs explicit formulation; neither term should silently become identical with the other.

[`temporal-experience-across-the-hypostatic-lattice.md`](https://github.com/KannonZenetism/zenetism-field-physics/blob/b5869dbceb834ba180517d750795e71a2c3bfee1/structural-physics/spx/temporal-experience-across-the-hypostatic-lattice.md#L398) infers C₁'s absence from L₁ from its omission in the Inlay, then explains embodied clock time through C₄ / C₂. ## What would establish a defensible mapping

Each proposed placement needs:

1. A stable definition of the operator's action
2. The hypostatic function that supports that action
3. A distinction between native or primary operation, additional coupling, and observed expression
4. A reason for any primary register or endpoint
5. An account of the operator's action across interfaces
6. A separate justification for the entropic counterpart
7. Reconciliation with the other recorded map and with broader space/time accounts

For a mathematical realization, domain, codomain, and action must also be specified. An illustrative coupling can be retained without being promoted to a universal assignment.
