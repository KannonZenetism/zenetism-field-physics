## Axiom of Necessity contra Probability in Multiversal Emergence

Earlier formulations claimed the multiverse as logically necessary given Zero / Infinity mathematical equivalence and the Principle of Sufficient Reason. This has been refined:

The distinct, co-present Aionic and Khaonic root-registers may admit **internal structural constraints** — not all logically possible configurations actualize. Examples:
- Non-fusion Axiom excludes certain combinations
- Coherence requirements filter structural incompatibilities  
- Priority structures (early actualization constrains later possibility)

Thus: Zero / Infinity equivalence + PSR **strongly indicate** multiversal emergence without requiring absolute logical necessity. The multiverse remains structurally probable but not metaphysically necessary.

This expresses Zenetism's epistemic standard: distinguish phenomenologically observable structure (hypostatic layers) from mathematically inferred cosmology (multiversal architecture).
