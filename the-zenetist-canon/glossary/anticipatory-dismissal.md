# Anticipatory Dismissal

**Anticipatory Dismissal** is the rejection, reduction, or defensive containment of a proposition before its actual evidentiary or structural case has been evaluated.

Rather than engaging what the argument establishes, the interpreter anticipates what category of claim it might become, what implications it might carry, or what consequences accepting it might produce, and responds to that anticipated form in advance.

The sequence is:

**proposition → anticipated implication → defensive response → premature rejection or reduction**

The originating claim therefore goes unexamined while the response addresses an expectation concerning what the claim is likely to mean.

Anticipatory Dismissal may operate through:

* premature qualification
* categorical reduction
* semantic substitution
* evidentiary standards imported before the claim's native register is established
* rejection based on anticipated implications rather than demonstrated error

Where **Preemptive Apologetics** occurs within the presentation of an argument, **Anticipatory Dismissal** occurs in its reception.

The corrective principle is:

> **Determine what the proposition actually establishes before responding to what its acceptance might imply.**
