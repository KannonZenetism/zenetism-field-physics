# Structural Thermodynamics

**Structural Thermodynamics** is the Zenetist cross-domain account of centropic and entropic orientation and motion as those relations are articulated across metaphysical and physical domains.

It treats thermodynamic, statistical-mechanical, information-theoretic, and other entropy quantities according to their own domain definitions, then relates them to structural orientation through explicitly established correspondences or domain realizations.

Centropy and Zenetist entropy are distinct orientations rather than opposite magnitudes on a single entropy scale. **Centropy** names integrative structuring motion characterized by lawful integration, coherent differentiation, synthesis, and reintegration with Plenary Zero. **Entropy** names dispersive fragmenting motion characterized by fragmentation, disintegration, entropic externalization, and terminal collapse.

Structural Thermodynamics therefore examines not only how a selected entropy quantity changes, but what a process integrates, preserves, replenishes, transfers, constrains, fragments, exhausts, or makes possible next.

Its focused Structural Physics articulation is `low-entropy-is-not-centropy.md`.
