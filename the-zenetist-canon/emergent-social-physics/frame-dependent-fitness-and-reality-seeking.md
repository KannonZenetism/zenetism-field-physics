## Definition

Fitness is never intelligible without a criterion of selection.

Biological fitness, social fitness, institutional fitness, and structural coherence do not measure the same thing. A trait may succeed within one frame while failing within another.

The error begins when the success-condition of one frame is treated as though it were an intrinsic measure of reality.

A social field may reward:

- conformity
- narrative agreement
- coalition stability
- identity preservation
- avoidance of disruptive disagreement

Within such a field, greater accuracy may carry social cost.

This does not mean that accuracy is intrinsically costly.

It means that the field imposes a cost upon whatever disrupts its preferred conditions of continuation.

---

## Cohesion Is Not Coherence

Social cohesion and structural coherence must remain distinct.

A group may be cohesive around an inaccurate account.

A tradition may remain stable because its assumptions are not examined.

A relationship may preserve surface harmony by suppressing unresolved contradiction.

In each case, cohesion has been maintained without establishing coherence.

The existence of social agreement therefore does not establish the adequacy of what is agreed upon.

Nor does social penalty establish that the person receiving it is structurally mistaken.

The penalty may reveal only that the prevailing field rewards preservation of its own frame.

---

## Reality-Seeking

**Reality-seeking** names the disposition to prefer increasingly adequate apprehension over the preservation of a comfortable but inadequate frame.

It does not require hostility toward relationship, association, or harmony.

It refuses only **harmony purchased through the concealment of substantive contradiction**.

For the reality-seeking posture, correction is not inherently a threat.

Loss of an inadequate account may be preferable to stability within it.

The relevant distinction is therefore not:

**individual contra group**

but:

**reality-responsive coherence contra frame-preserving cohesion**

A coherent social field need not fear examination.

A field dependent upon unexamined assumptions may experience examination itself as destabilizing.

---

## Frame-Relative Cost

When accuracy produces isolation, conflict, exclusion, or loss of status, the cost should be located correctly.

The cost does not arise from accuracy as such.

It arises from the relation between accuracy and a field whose continuation depends upon some incompatible commitment.

Thus:

> What a field punishes reveals what the field is organized to preserve.

This does not establish that every dissenter is accurate.

Dissent has no automatic centropic status.

It establishes only that conformity and accuracy are independent variables.

A Zenetist social analysis must therefore evaluate the coherence of the relation itself rather than infer validity from either consensus or opposition.

---

## Principle

**Frame-Dependent Fitness Principle**

> A being, belief, or behavior is "fit" only relative to the conditions by which a given field selects for continuation. Social fitness therefore cannot by itself establish structural coherence, and social cost cannot by itself establish structural error.

Reality-seeking begins where the selection criteria themselves become available for examination.
