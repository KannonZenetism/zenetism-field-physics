## About this deposit

This deposit carries *Zenetist Structural Decodes* — the complete collection of 32 numbered and named archetypal / mytho-religious structural decodes within the Zenetist Canon. The documents examine religious, mythological, and archetypal narratives through hypostatic relations, directional motion, and glyphic articulation.

The collection belongs to Mythostructural Forensics, a subfield of Structural Forensics. Structural Mythophysics articulates the metaphysical architecture refracted in myth; Mythostructural Forensics examines how narrative and symbol encode, preserve, distort, or conflate that structure.
