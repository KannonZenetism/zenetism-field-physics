## Overview

*Zenetist Structural Decodes* comprises 32 numbered and named archetypal / mytho-religious structural decodes within the Zenetist Canon. Each document examines a particular narrative motif through hypostatic relations, directional motion, and glyphic articulation.

The collection belongs to Mythostructural Forensics, a subfield of Structural Forensics. This diagnostic practice examines religious, mythological, and archetypal narratives as encodings of metaphysical structure. It traces how narrative and symbol express, preserve, distort, or conflate that structure across distinct traditions.

Structural Mythophysics articulates the metaphysical architecture refracted in myth. Mythostructural Forensics carries out the diagnostic reading of its narrative forms. The decodes bring that relation into examinations of centropic / entropic dynamics, hypostatic attribution, and structural pattern.
