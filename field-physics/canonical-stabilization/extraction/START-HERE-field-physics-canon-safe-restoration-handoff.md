1. restore the missing underlying architecture in current terminology
2. preserve the developmental definition in a historical note

1. identify the source-era meaning of each glyph
2. identify the current meaning of each glyph
3. test for collision, reassignment, provisional status, or multi-application

5. archive or hold the formula where it cannot be translated lawfully

A source glyph must never be assumed to retain its old meaning.

## `LOCK-NO-005` — No Silent Cross-File Propagation

A repair to one FP volume must not rewrite another FP volume automatically.

Record cross-file impacts in a propagation list for later approved repair.

## `LOCK-NO-006` — Additive Restoration before Restructuring

- inserting a missing bridge

- adding a table or registry

## `LOCK-NO-008` — No Automatic "Cleanup" of Metaphysical Register

## `LOCK-NO-009` — Formal Appearance Does Not Establish Formal Standing

Formal results state their assumptions and valid calculation or proof; empirical applications specify their observables, correspondence, measurement procedure, and supporting evidence.
